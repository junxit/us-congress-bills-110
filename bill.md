# H.R. 2435

> To amend the Occupational Safety and Health Act to provide for criminal liability for willful safety standard violations resulting in the death of contract employees.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Equal Protection for All Workers Act”.

## § 2. Definition of employee

Section 17(e) of the Occupational Safety and Health Act (29 U.S.C. 666(e)) is amended by inserting “including any individual employed under contract,” after “employee,”.
