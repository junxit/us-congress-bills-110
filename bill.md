# H.R. 3594

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent law the penalty-free distributions from retirement plans to individuals called to active duty.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “America's Heroes Tax Relief Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Penalty-free distributions from retirement plans to individuals called to active duty made permanent law

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (G) of section 72(t)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking clause (iv).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to distributions after December 31, 2007.
