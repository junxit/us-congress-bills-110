# S. 678

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to ensure air passengers have access to necessary services while on a grounded air carrier and are not unnecessarily held on a grounded air carrier before or after a flight, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Airline Passenger Bill of Rights Act of 2007”.
