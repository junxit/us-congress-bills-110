# H.R. 1936

> To amend the Small Business Act to increase the minimum Government-wide goal for procurement contracts awarded to small business concerns.

## § 1. Increase in government-wide goal for procurement contracts awarded to small business concerns

Section 15(g)(1) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 644(g)(1)) is amended by striking “23 percent” and inserting “25 percent”.
