# S.J. RES. 6

> Providing for the reappointment of Walter E. Massey as a citizen regent of the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

That, in accordance with section 5581 of the Revised Statutes (20 U.S.C. 43), the vacancy on the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, in the class other than Members of Congress, occurring because of the expiration of the term of Walter E. Massey of Georgia, is filled by the reappointment of Walter E. Massey, for a term of 6 years, effective March 17, 2007.
