# H. CON. RES. 110

> Expressing the sense of Congress that Iraq should vote to approve or disapprove the continued deployment of United States Armed Forces to Iraq and, unless Iraq votes to approve such continued deployment, the President of the United States should commence the phased redeployment of United States Armed Forces from Iraq within 60 days of the Iraqi vote.

That it is the sense of Congress that—

- **(1)** the Government of Iraq should hold a vote in the Iraqi Council of Representatives or among the Iraqi general voting public to approve or disapprove the continued deployment of United States Armed Forces to Iraq; and
- **(2)** unless 60 percent of the members of the Iraqi Council of Representatives or the Iraqi general voting public vote to approve the continued deployment of United States Armed Forces to Iraq, the President of the United States should commence the phased redeployment of United States Armed Forces from Iraq within 60 days of the Iraqi vote.
