# S. 853

> To deauthorize the project for navigation, Northeast Harbor, Maine.

## § 1. Northeast harbor, Maine

The project for navigation, Northeast Harbor, Maine, authorized by section 2 of the Act of March 2, 1945 (59 Stat. 12, chapter 19), is deauthorized as of the date of enactment of this Act.
