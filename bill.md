# S. 961

> To amend title 46, United States Code, to provide benefits to certain individuals who served in the United States merchant marine (including the Army Transport Service and the Naval Transport Service) during World War II, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Belated Thank You to the Merchant Mariners of World War II Act of 2007”.
