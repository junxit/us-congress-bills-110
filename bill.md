# H.R. 7182

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to include in the definition of the term “aggravated felony” a criminal violation committed by an alien who unlawfully entered the United States.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Two Strike Act”.

## § 2. Criminal violations by aliens entering unlawfully

Section 101(a)(43) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(43)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “and” at the end of subparagraph (T);
- **(2)** by striking the period and inserting “; and” at the end of subparagraph (U); and
- **(3)** by inserting after subparagrah (U) the following:
  > - **(V)** an offense committed in the United States for which a term of imprisonment may be imposed after the date of the enactment of this subparagraph by an alien who violated section 275(a).
