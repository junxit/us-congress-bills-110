# S. 3485

> To require manufacturers to increase the percentage of automobiles manufactured for sale within the United States that are capable of operating on higher-level blends of renewable fuels, such as ethanol and biodiesel, in combination with gasoline or diesel fuel.

  > - **(b)** *Exception* Subsection (a) shall not apply to automobiles that operate only on electricity.
