# H.R. 2845

> To amend the State Department Basic Authorities Act of 1956 and the Foreign Service Act of 1980 to enable the Secretary of State to respond to a critical shortage of passport processing personnel.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Department of State Crisis Response Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Reemployment of civil service annuitants

Section 61(a) of the State Department Basic Authorities Act of 1956 (22 U.S.C. 2733(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1), by striking “To facilitate” and all that follows through “the Secretary” and inserting “The Secretary”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (2), by striking “2008” and inserting “2010”.

## § 3. Reemployment of Foreign Service annuitants

Section 824(g) of the Foreign Service Act of 1980 (22 U.S.C. 4064(g)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1)(B), by striking “to facilitate” and all that follows through “Afghanistan,”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (2), in the first sentence, by striking “2008” and inserting “2010”.
