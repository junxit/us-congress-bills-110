# H.R. 5294

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the additional 0.2 percent FUTA surtax.

## § 1. Repeal of the additional 0.2 percent FUTA surtax and rebate of payroll taxes paid in calendar year 2008

- **(a)** *In general* Section 3301 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to rate of tax) is amended by striking “equal to” and all that follows through “of the total wages” and inserting “equal to 6.0 percent of the total wages”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to remuneration paid after December 31, 2007.
