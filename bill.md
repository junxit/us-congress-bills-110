# H.R. 1272

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to improve the pension program of the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Veterans' Pension Improvement Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Improvement to veterans pension program

- **(a)** *Veterans pension* Section 1521 of title 38, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (b), by striking “$3,550” and inserting “$12,250”;
  - **(2)** in subsection (c), by striking “$4,651” and “$600” and inserting “$16,500” and “$2,088”, respectively;
  - **(3)** in subsection (d)—
    - **(A)** in paragraph (1), by striking “$5,680” and inserting “$19,322”; and
    - **(B)** in paragraph (2), by striking “$6,781” and “$600” and inserting “$23,572” and “$2,088”, respectively;
  - **(4)** in subsection (e), by striking “$4,340”, “$5,441”, and “$600” and inserting “$14,606”, “$18,856”, and “$2,088”, respectively;
  - **(5)** in subsection (f)—
    - **(A)** in paragraph (1), by striking “$4,651” and inserting “$16,500”;
    - **(B)** in paragraph (2), by striking “$6,781” and “$8,911” and inserting “$23,572” and “$30,644”, respectively;
    - **(C)** in paragraph (3), by striking “$5,441” and “$6,231” and inserting “$18,856” and “$21,212”, respectively;
    - **(D)** in paragraph (4), by striking “$7,571” and inserting “$25,928”; and
    - **(E)** in paragraph (5), by striking “$600” and inserting “$2,088”; and
  - **(6)** in subsection (g), by striking “$800” and inserting “$2,400”.
- **(b)** *Surviving spouse pension* Section 1541 of such title is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (b), by striking “$2,379” and inserting “$11,760”;
  - **(2)** in subsection (c), by striking “$3,116” and “$600” and inserting “$15,840” and “$2,088”, respectively;
  - **(3)** in subsection (d)—
    - **(A)** in paragraph (1), by striking “$3,806” and inserting “$16,006”; and
    - **(B)** in paragraph (2), by striking “$4,543” and “$600” and inserting “$20,086” and “$2,088”, respectively; and
  - **(4)** in subsection (e)(1), by striking “$2,908”, “$3,645”, and “$600” and inserting “$13,336”, “$17,416”, and “$2,088”, respectively.
- **(c)** *Child pension* Section 1542 of such title is amended by striking “$600” both places it appears and inserting “$2,088”.
- **(d)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall take effect on December 1, 2007.
- **(e)** *Prevention of duplicate increase* During fiscal year 2008, no increase shall be made pursuant to section 5312(a) of title 38, United States Code, in any dollar amount specified in section 1521 (other than subsection (g)), section 1541, or section 1542 of such title.
