# H.R. 5459

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on Triadimefon.

## § 1. Triadimefon

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.02.33 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “Free” and inserting “0.5%”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2006” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
