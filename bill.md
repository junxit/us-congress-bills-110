# S. 3624

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to require States and metropolitan planning organizations to develop transportation greenhouse gas reduction plans to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from the transportation sector, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Clean, Low-Emission, Affordable, New Transportation Efficiency Act”.
