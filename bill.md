# H. CON. RES. 28

> Expressing the sense of the Congress that a commemorative postage stamp should be issued to promote public awareness of Down syndrome.

That it is the sense of the Congress that—

- **(1)** the United States Postal Service should issue a commemorative postage stamp to promote public awareness of Down syndrome; and
- **(2)** the Citizens’ Stamp Advisory Committee of the United States Postal Service should recommend to the Postmaster General that such a stamp be issued.
