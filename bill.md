# S. 2615

> To extend the Protect America Act of 2007 for 15 days.

## § 1. 15-day extension of the Protect America Act of 2007

Section 6(c) of the Protect America Act of 2007 (Public Law 110–55; 121 Stat. 557; 50 U.S.C. 1803 note) is amended by striking “195 days” and inserting “210 days”.
