# H. RES. 1092

> Relating to the consideration of the bill (H.R. 5724) to implement the United States-Colombia Trade Promotion Agreement.

That section 151(e)(1) and section 151(f)(1) of the Trade Act of 1974 shall not apply in the case of the bill (H.R. 5724) to implement the United States-Colombia Trade Promotion Agreement.
