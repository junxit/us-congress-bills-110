# H.R. 5888

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to expand veteran eligibility for reimbursement by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs for emergency treatment furnished in a non-Department facility.

## § 1. Expansion of veteran eligibility for reimbursement by Secretary of Veterans Affairs for emergency treatment furnished in a non-Department facility

- **(a)** *Expansion of eligibility* Section 1725(b)(3)(C) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “or in part”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to emergency treatment furnished on or after October 8, 2007.
