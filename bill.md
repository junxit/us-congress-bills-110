# H.R. 4707

> To reliquidate certain entries of gemifloxacin.

## § 1. Reliquidation of certain entries of gemifloxacin

- **(a)** *In general* Notwithstanding section 514 of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. 1514) or any other provision of law, U.S. Customs and Border Protection shall, not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act—
  - **(1)** reliquidate each entry described in subsection (c) containing any merchandise that, on the date of original liquidation, was classified under subheading 2933.99.46 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States; and
  - **(2)** refund any duty paid for such entries under subheading 2933.99.46 of such Schedule.
- **(b)** *Refund of Amounts Owed* Any amounts owed by the United States under subsection (a) shall be refunded with interest.
