# H.R. 1248

> To amend section 10501 of title 49, United States Code, to exclude solid waste disposal from the jurisdiction of the Surface Transportation Board.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Clean Railroads Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Amendments to exclude solid waste facilities from the jurisdiction of the Board

Section 10501 of title 49, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “facilities,” in subsection (b)(2) and inserting “facilities (except solid waste management facilities (as defined in section 1004 of the Solid Waste Disposal Act (42 U.S.C. 6903))),”; and
- **(2)** by striking “over mass transportation provided by a local governmental authority.” in subsection (c)(2) and inserting
  > over—
  > - **(A)** mass transportation provided by a local governmental authority; or
  > - **(B)** the processing or sorting of solid waste.
