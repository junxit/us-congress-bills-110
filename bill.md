# S. 3721

> To prohibit the use of funds to transfer individuals detained by the United States at Naval Station, Guantanamo Bay, Cuba, to the United States Disciplinary Barracks, Fort Leavenworth, Kansas.

## § 1. Prohibition on use of funds to transfer individuals detained at Guantanamo Bay, Cuba, to the United States Disciplinary Barracks, Fort Leavenworth, Kansas

None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made available to any Federal department or agency may be used to transfer any individual detained by the United States at Naval Station, Guantanamo Bay, Cuba, to the United States Disciplinary Barracks, Fort Leavenworth, Kansas.
