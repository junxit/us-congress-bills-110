# S. 1429

> To amend the Safe Drinking Water Act to reauthorize the provision of technical assistance to small public water systems.

## § 1. Technical assistance

Section 1442(e) of the Safe Drinking Water Act (42 U.S.C. 300j–1(e)) is amended in the fifth sentence by striking “1997 through 2003” and inserting “2008 through 2012”.
