# H.R. 1644

> To amend the National Labor Relations Act to clarify the definition of “supervisor” for purposes of such Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Re-Empowerment of Skilled and Professional Employees and Construction Tradesworkers (RESPECT) Act”.

## § 2. Definition of supervisor

Section 2(11) of the National Labor Relations Act (29 U.S.C. 152(11)) is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “and for a majority of the individual’s worktime” after “interest of the employer”;
- **(2)** by striking “assign,”; and
- **(3)** by striking “or responsibility to direct them,”.
