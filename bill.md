# H.R. 6049

That the bill from the House of Representatives (H.R. 6049) entitled “An Act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide incentives for energy production and conservation, to extend certain expiring provisions, to provide individual income tax relief, and for other purposes.”, do pass with the following

*Strike all after the enacting clause and insert the following:*

## § 1. Short title, etc

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Energy Improvement and Extension Act of 2008”.
- **(b)** *Reference* Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
