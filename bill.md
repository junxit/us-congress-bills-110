# S. 343

> To extend the District of Columbia College Access Act of 1999.

## § 1. 5-year reauthorization of tuition assistance programs

- **(a)** *Public school program* Section 3(i) of the District of Columbia College Access Act of 1999 (sec. 38–2702(i), D.C. Official Code) is amended by striking “each of the 7 succeeding fiscal years” and inserting “each of the 12 succeeding fiscal years”.
- **(b)** *Private school program* Section 5(f) of such Act (sec. 38–2704(f), D.C. Official Code) is amended by striking “each of the 7 succeeding fiscal years” and inserting “each of the 12 succeeding fiscal years”.
