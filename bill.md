# H.R. 5483

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 10449 White Granite Drive in Oakton, Virginia, as the “Private First Class David H. Sharrett II Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Private First Class David H. Sharrett II Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 10449 White Granite Drive in Oakton, Virginia, shall be known and designated as the “Private First Class David H. Sharrett II Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Private First Class David H. Sharrett II Post Office Building”.
