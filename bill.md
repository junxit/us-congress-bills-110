# H.R. 5330

> To provide for the liquidation or reliquidation of certain entries of bulk aspirin.

## § 1. Certain entries of bulk aspirin

- **(a)** *Liquidation or reliquidation of entries* Notwithstanding sections 514 and 520 of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. 1514 and 1520) or any other provision of law, the United States Customs and Border Protection Service shall, not later than 90 days after the date of enactment of this Act, liquidate or reliquidate the entries listed in subsection (b) without assessment of antidumping duties or interest and shall refund any antidumping duties or interest which were previously paid.
