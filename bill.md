# H.R. 3284

> To amend title 49, United States Code, by repealing the provision regarding the acquisition management system for the Transportation Security Administration.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “TSA Acquisition Reform Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Repeal

- **(a)** *In general* Section 114 of title 49, United States Code, is amended by repealing subsection (o).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act.
