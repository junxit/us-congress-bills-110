# H.R. 2527

> To provide for enhanced protection of the Internal Revenue Service and employees of the Internal Revenue Service.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Enhanced Protection of the Internal Revenue Service and Its Employees Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Enhanced protection of the Internal Revenue Service and its employees

Section 8D(k)(1)(C) of the Inspector General Act of 1978 is amended by striking “and the providing of physical security”.
