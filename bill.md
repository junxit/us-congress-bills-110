# H.J. RES. 79

> Disapproving the rule submitted by the Federal Communications Commission with respect to broadcast media ownership.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Federal Communications Commission relating to broadcast media ownership (Report and Order FCC 07–216), received by Congress on February 22, 2008, and such rule shall have no force or effect.
