# H.R. 6894

> To extend and reauthorize the Defense Production Act of 1950, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Defense Production Act Extension and Reauthorization of 2008”.

## § 2. Extension of termination date

Section 717(a) of the Defense Production Act of 1950 (50 U.S.C. App. 2166(a)) is amended by striking “September 30, 2008” and inserting “September 30, 2009”.

## § 3. Reauthorization

Section 711(b) of the Defense Production Act of 1950 (50 U.S.C. App. 2161(b)) is amended by striking “2008” and inserting “2009”.
