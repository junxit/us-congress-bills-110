# H.R. 3099

> To amend title 10, to permit members of the Selected Reserves and members of the reserve component to renter their military service and receive chapter 1607 education benefits if that member has earned such benefit before originally separating from service.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Guard and Reserve Higher Education Fairness Act”.

## § 2. Permit members of the Selected Reserves and members of the reserve component to renter their military service and receive chapter 1607 education benefits if that member has earned such benefit before originally separating from service

- **(a)** *Members of selected reserves* Section 16165(b) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking “of not more than 90 days” after “who incurs a break in service in the Selected Reserves”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply immediately upon enactment into law.
