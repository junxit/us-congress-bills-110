# H.R. 1948

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to increase the amount of additional compensation payable to an employee who is disabled and requires the services of an attendant, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Joseph E. Keane Federal Employees Freedom Act”.

## § 2. Increase in amount

Section 8111(a) of title 5, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “$1,500” and inserting “$4,000”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following: “The additional sum allowable under this subsection shall be increased, beginning October 1, 2009, and biennially thereafter, by the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (United States city average) during the 2-year period ending 2 months before the date of the increase.”.
