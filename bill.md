# H.R. 4117

> To suspend temporarily the duty on certain electronic dimming ballasts with a three wire control scheme.

## § 1. Temporary suspension of duty

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by inserting in numerical sequence the following new heading:
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
