# H.R. 7098

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income discharges of student loans the repayment of which is income contingent or income based.

## § 1. Certain student loans the repayment of which is income contingent or income based

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (1) of section 108(f) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “any student loan if” and all that follows and inserting
  > any student loan if—
  > - **(A)** such discharge was pursuant to a provision of such loan under which all or part of the indebtedness of the individual would be discharged if the individual worked for a certain period of time in certain professions for any of a broad class of employers, or
  > - **(B)** such discharge was pursuant to section 455(e) or section 493C(b)(7) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (relating to income contingent and income based repayment).

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to discharges of loans after December 31, 2008.
