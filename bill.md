# S. 2336

> To designate the Port Angeles Federal Building in Port Angeles, Washington, as the “Richard B. Anderson Federal Building”.

## § 1. Richard B. Anderson Federal Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The Federal building located at 138 West First Street, Port Angeles, Washington, shall be known and designated as the “Richard B. Anderson Federal Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Richard B. Anderson Federal Building”.
