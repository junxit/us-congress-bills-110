# S. 854

> To modify the project for navigation, Union River, Maine.

## § 1. Union River, Maine

The project for navigation, Union River, Maine, authorized by the first section of the Act of June 3, 1896 (29 Stat. 215, chapter 314), is modified by redesignating as an anchorage area that portion of the project consisting of a 6-foot turning basin and lying northerly of a line commencing at a point N 315,975.13, E 1,004,424.86, thence running north 61 degrees 27 minutes 20.71 seconds west about 132.34 feet to a point N 316,038.37, E 1,004,308.61.
