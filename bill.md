# S. 3582

> To require continued application of budget neutrality on a national basis in calculation of the Medicare urban hospital wage floor.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Safeguarding Our Hospitals’ Futures Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Continued application of budget neutrality on a national basis in calculation of the Medicare urban hospital wage floor

In the case of discharges occurring on or after October 1, 2008, the Secretary of Health and Human Services shall continue to administer section 4410(b) of the Balanced Budget Act of 1997 (42 U.S.C. 1395ww note) and section 412.64(e) of title 42, Code of Federal Regulations, in the same manner as the Secretary administered such sections for discharges occurring during fiscal year 2008 (through a uniform, national adjustment to the area wage index).
