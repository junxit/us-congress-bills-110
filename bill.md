# S. 1110

> To amend the Reclamation Projects Authorization and Adjustment Act of 1992 to provide for the conjunctive use of surface and ground water in Juab County, Utah.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Juab County Surface and Ground Water Study and Development Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Conjunctive use of surface and ground water, central utah project

Section 202(a)(2) of the Reclamation Projects Authorization and Adjustment Act of 1992 (Public Law 102–575; 106 Stat. 4609) is amended by inserting “Juab,” after “Davis,”.
