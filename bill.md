# H.R. 2909

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to restore the deduction for the travel expenses of a taxpayer’s spouse who accompanies the taxpayer on business travel.

## § 1. Restoration of deduction for travel expenses of spouse, etc. accompanying taxpayer on business travel

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (m) of section 274 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to additional limitations on travel expenses) is amended by striking paragraph (3).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to amounts paid or incurred after the date of the enactment of this Act.
