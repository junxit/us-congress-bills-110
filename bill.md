# S. 1728

> To amend the National Parks and Recreation Act of 1978 to reauthorize the Na Hoa Pili O Kaloko-Honokohau Advisory Commission.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Na Hoa Pili O Kaloko-Honokohau Advisory Commission Reauthorization Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Na Hoa Pili O Kaloko-Honokohau advisory commission

Section 505(f)(7) of the National Parks and Recreation Act of 1978 (16 U.S.C. 396d(f)(7)) is amended by striking “ten years after the date of enactment of the Na Hoa Pili Kaloko-Honokohau Re-establishment Act of 1996” and inserting “on Decmber 31, 2017”.
