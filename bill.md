# H.R. 5396

> To designate the Cold War Museum in Fairfax, Virginia, as the National Cold War Museum.

## § 1. National Cold War Museum

- **(a)** *Designation* The Cold War Museum in Fairfax, Virginia, shall be known and designated as the “National Cold War Museum”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the museum referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the National Cold War Museum.
