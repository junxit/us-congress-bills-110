# H.R. 4376

> To extend the suspension of duty on Styrene, ar-ethyl-, polymer with divinylbenzene and styrene beads with low ash.

## § 1. Styrene, ar-ethyl-, polymer with di­vi­nyl­ben­zene and styrene beads with low ash

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.25.40 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Styrene, ar-ethyl-, polymer with divinylbenzene and styrene beads with low ash) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
