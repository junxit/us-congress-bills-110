# H.R. 612

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend the period of eligibility for health care for combat service in the Persian Gulf War or future hostilities from two years to five years after discharge or release.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Returning Servicemember VA Healthcare Insurance Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Extension of period of eligibility for health care for combat service in the Persian Gulf War or future hostilities

Section 1710(e)(3)(C) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “2 years” and inserting “5 years”.
