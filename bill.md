# S. 3497

> To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to decrease the period of benefit ineligibility of certain adults due to unemployment.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Food Insecurity Reduction Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Period of ineligibility for supplemental nutrition assistance due to unemployment

- **(a)** *In general* Section 6(o) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2015(o)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in paragraph (2), by striking “3 months” and inserting “12 months”,
  - **(2)** in paragraph (5)(C), by striking “3-month” each place it appears and inserting “12-month”, and
  - **(3)** in paragraph (6)(A)(ii)(IV), by striking “3 months” and inserting “12 months”.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Section 16(h)(1)(E)(ii)(I) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2025(h)(1)(E)(ii)(I)) is amended by striking “3-month” and inserting “12-month”.
