# S. 1039

> To extend the authorization for the Coastal Heritage Trail in the State of New Jersey.

## § 1. Extension of authorization

Section 6(c) of Public Law 100–515 (16 U.S.C. 1244 note) is amended by striking “shall terminate on September 30, 2007” and inserting “shall terminate on September 30, 2011”.
