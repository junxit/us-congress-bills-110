# H.R. 5574

> To prohibit the transport of hydrolysate from the Pueblo Chemical Depot, Colorado, or the Blue Grass Army Depot, Kentucky, to an off-site location.

## § 1. Prohibition on transport of hydrolysate at certain Army depots

- **(a)** *Prohibition* The Secretary of Defense may not transport hydrolysate from the Pueblo Chemical Depot, Colorado, or the Blue Grass Army Depot, Kentucky, to an off-site location for treatment, storage, or disposal.
- **(b)** *Savings clause* Nothing in this Act limits or otherwise affects section 8119 of the Department of Defense Appropriations Act, 2008 (Public Law 110–116; 50 U.S.C. 1521 note).
