# S. 1917

> To include Idaho and Montana as affected areas for purposes of making claims under the Radiation Exposure Compensation Act (42 U.S.C. 2210 note) based on exposure to atmospheric nuclear testing.

## § 1. Inclusion of Idaho and Montana in radiation exposure compensation

Section 4(b)(1) of the Radiation Exposure Compensation Act (42 U.S.C. 2210 note) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “and” after the semicolon;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (C), by striking “and” after the semicolon; and
- **(3)** by adding after subparagraph (C) the following:
  > - **(D)** the State of Idaho; and
  > - **(E)** the State of Montana; and
