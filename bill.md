# S. 3583

> To limit or deny civil service protection for a Federal employee if the appointment of that employee is a prohibited personnel practice that was made on the basis of political affiliation as prohibited under any law, rule, or regulation.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Political Independence of the Civil Service Act of 2008”.
