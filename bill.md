# H.R. 4136

> To amend title 18 of the United States Code to clarify the scope of the child pornography laws, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the “Enhancing the Effective Prosecution of Child Pornography Act of 2007.”

## § 2. Money Laundering Predicate

Section 1956(c)(7)(D) of title 18, United States Code, is amended by inserting “2252A (relating to child pornography) where the child pornography at issue involves minors, 2260 (production of certain child pornography for importation into the United States), before “section 2280”.”

## § 3. Knowingly Accessing Child Pornography

- **(a)** Section 2252(a)(4) of title 18, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by inserting after “any visual depiction” the following: “or knowingly accesses child pornography on the internet with intent to view it”;
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (B), by inserting after “including by computer” the following: “or knowingly accesses child pornography on the internet with intent to view it”; and
- **(b)** Section 2252A(5) of title 18, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by inserting after “that contains any image of child pornography” the following: “or knowingly accesses child pornography on the internet with intent to view it”; and
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (B), by inserting after “including by computer” the following: “or knowingly accesses child pornography on the internet with intent to view it”.
