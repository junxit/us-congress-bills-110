# H.R. 3145

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to increase criminal penalties for certain removed aliens who illegally reenter the United States.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Prohibition Against Criminal Alien Reentry Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Increased criminal penalties for certain removed aliens

Section 276(b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1326(b)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1)—
  - **(A)** by striking “three” and inserting “two”, and
  - **(B)** by striking “10” and inserting “20”;
- **(2)** in paragraph (2), by striking “20” and inserting “30”;
- **(3)** in paragraph (3), by striking “10” and inserting “20”; and
- **(4)** in paragraph (4), by striking “10” and inserting “20”.
