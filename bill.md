# S. 1703

> To prevent and reduce trafficking in persons.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Trafficking in Persons Accountability Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Jurisdiction in certain trafficking offenses

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 77 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > ## § 1596. Jurisdiction in certain trafficking offenses
  >
  > The jurisdiction of the courts of the United States over any offense under this chapter shall include any case in which, not later than 10 years after the conduct required for the offense occurs, the alleged offender is brought into, or found in, the United States, even if that conduct occurred outside the United States.
