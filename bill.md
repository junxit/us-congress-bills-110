# H.R. 3970

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide additional tax relief to low and moderate income individuals, to repeal the individual alternative minimum tax, to reform the corporate income tax, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title, etc

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Tax Reduction and Reform Act of 2007”.
- **(b)** *Reference* Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
