# S. 2922

> To repeal certain oil and gas incentives established in the Energy Policy Act of 2005, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Repeal of certain oil and gas incentives

Sections 344 and 345 of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 (42 U.S.C. 15904, 15905) are repealed.
