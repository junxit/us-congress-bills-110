# H.R. 208

> To amend the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act to expand the fruit and vegetable pilot program to 5 States, including New York, and to include Head Start programs.

## § 1. Eligibility for fruit and vegetable pilot program

Section 18(g)(1)(B) of the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act (42 U.S.C. 1769(g)(1)(B)) is amended by striking “in each of 4 States (including a State” and inserting “or Head Start programs authorized under the Head Start Act (42 U.S.C. 9831 et seq.), in each of 5 States (including New York and a State”.
