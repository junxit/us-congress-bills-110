# H.R. 4948

> To extend the duty suspension on Crotonaldehyde (2-butenaldehyde).

## § 1. Crotonaldehyde (2-butenaldehyde)

- **(a)** *In General* Heading 9902.10.44 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Crotonaldehyde (2-butenaldehyde)) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective Date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the date that is 15 days after the date of enactment of this Act.
