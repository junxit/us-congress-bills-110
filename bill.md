# H. RES. 244

> Electing members to the Joint Committee on Printing and the Joint Committee of Congress on the Library.

## § 1. Election of Members to Joint Committee on Printing and Joint Committee of Congress on the Library

- **(a)** *Joint Committee on Printing* The following Members are hereby elected to the Joint Committee on Printing, to serve with the chair of the Committee on House Administration:
  - **(1)** Mr. Brady of Pennsylvania.
  - **(2)** Mr. Capuano.
  - **(3)** Mr. Ehlers.
  - **(4)** Mr. McCarthy of California.
- **(b)** *Joint Committee of Congress on the Library* The following Members are hereby elected to the Joint Committee of Congress on the Library, to serve with the chair of the Committee on House Administration:
  - **(1)** Ms. Zoe Lofgren of California.
  - **(2)** Mr. Ehlers.
  - **(3)** Mr. Daniel E. Lungren of California.
