# H.R. 1777

> To amend the Improving America’s Schools Act of 1994 to make permanent the favorable treatment of need-based educational aid under the antitrust laws.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Need-Based Educational Aid Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Amendment

Section 568(d) of the Improving America’s Schools Act of 1994 (15 U.S.C. 1 note) is amended by striking “2008” and inserting “2015”.
