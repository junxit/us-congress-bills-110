# H.R. 4526

> To extend temporarily the duty on Dimethyl Carbonate, CAS Number 616–38–6.

## § 1. Temporary extension of duty

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.12.43 (pertaining to dimethyl carbonate) is amended by striking the date in the effective date column and inserting “12/31/11” in lieu thereof.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
