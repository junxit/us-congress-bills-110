# H.R. 3113

> To clarify congressional intent with respect to the provision of affordable financial services by all types of Federal credit unions in underserved areas, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Affordable Financial Services Enhancement Act”.

## § 2. Affordable financial services for underserved areas

Section 109(c)(2) of the Federal Credit Union Act (12 U.S.C. 1759(c)(2)) is amended by striking “subsection (b)(2)” and inserting “any paragraph of subsection (b)”.
