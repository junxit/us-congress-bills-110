# H.R. 4307

> To amend the Communications Act of 1934, and increase consumer freedom of choice in the video marketplace.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Consumer Freedom of Choice in Cable Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of 70/70 Rule

Section 612 of the Communications Act of 1934 (47 U.S.C. 532) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking subsection (g);
- **(2)** in subsection (j)(1), by striking “subsection (h)” and inserting “subsection (g)”; and
- **(3)** by redesignating subsections (h), (i), and (j) as subsections (g), (h), and (i), respectively.
