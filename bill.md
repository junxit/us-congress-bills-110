# H.R. 4535

> To suspend temporarily the duty on 2-amino-5-chloro-N,3-dimethylbenzamide.

## § 1. Temporary suspension of duty

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by inserting in numerical sequence the following new heading:
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
