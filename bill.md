# H.R. 526

> To amend the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act to provide full funding for assistance for education of all children with disabilities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Full Funding for IDEA Now Act.”

## § 2. Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to attain the Federal Government’s goal under part B of the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (20 U.S.C. 1411 et seq.) of providing 40 percent of the national current average per pupil expenditure to assist States and local educational agencies with the excess costs of educating children with disabilities and to make such funding mandatory.
