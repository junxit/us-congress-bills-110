# H.R. 2933

> To amend the American Battlefield Protection Act of 1996 to extend the authorization for that Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Civil War Battlefield Preservation Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Authorization extended

The American Battlefield Protection Act of 1996 (16 U.S.C. 469k) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (d)(7)(A), by striking “fiscal years 2004 through 2008” and inserting “fiscal years 2009 through 2013”; and
- **(2)** by striking subsection (e).
