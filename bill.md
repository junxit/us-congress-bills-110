# H.R. 2194

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to authorize an allowance for civilian clothing for members of the Armed Forces traveling in connection with medical evacuation.

## § 1. Allowance for civilian clothing for members of the Armed Forces traveling in connection with medical evacuation

Section 1047(a) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by inserting “and luggage” after “civilian clothing” both places it appears.
