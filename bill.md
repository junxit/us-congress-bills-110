# H.R. 2828

> To provide compensation to relatives of United States citizens who were killed as a result of the bombings of United States Embassies in East Africa on August 7, 1998.

## § 1. Compensation for United States citizens who were victims of the bombings of United States embassies in East Africa on August 7, 1998

- **(a)** *Purpose* The purpose of this section is to provide compensation to relatives of United States citizens who were killed as a result of the bombings of United States Embassies in East Africa on August 7, 1998.
- **(b)** *Compensation authorized* The Secretary of State is authorized to provide compensation to the personal representative of a decedent who is an eligible individual described in subsection (c).
- **(c)** *Eligible individuals* A decedent is an eligible individual for purposes of this subsection if the individual was a citizen of the United States who—
  - **(1)** was present at the United States Embassy in Nairobi, Kenya, or the United States Embassy in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, at the time, or in the immediate aftermath, of the bombings of United States embassies in East Africa on August 7, 1998; and
  - **(2)** suffered death as a result of such a bombing.
- **(d)** *Equitable distribution* To the maximum extent practicable, the Secretary of State shall ensure an equitable distribution of amounts of compensation under this section among the personal representatives of decedents who are eligible individuals described in subsection (c).
- **(e)** *Regulations* The Secretary of State is authorized to promulgate such regulations as may be necessary to carry out this section.
- **(f)** *Authorization of appropriations* To carry out this section, there is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of State $18,000,000.
