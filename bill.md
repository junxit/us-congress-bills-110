# S. 3718

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to suspend the taxation of unemployment compensation for 2 years.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Unemployment Benefit Tax Suspension Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Suspension of tax on unemployment compensation

- **(a)** *In general* Section 85 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to unemployment compensation) is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection:
  > - **(c)** *Temporary suspension* Subsection (a) shall not apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2007, and before January 1, 2010.
