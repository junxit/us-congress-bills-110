# H.R. 5498

> To increase the cap on the obligation of receipts for the Crime Victims Fund.

## § 1. Increase of cap on the obligation of receipts for the Crime Victims Fund

Section 513 of Division B of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2008 (Public Law 110–161) is amended by striking “$590,000,000” and inserting “$661,000,000”.
