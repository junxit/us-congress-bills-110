# H.R. 3623

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the deduction for contributions of real property made for conservation purposes.

## § 1. Extension of deduction for qualified conservation contributions

- **(a)** *In general* Clause (vi) of section 170(b)(1)(E) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to termination of deduction for qualified conservation contributions) is amended by striking “December 31, 2007” and inserting “December 31, 2008”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to contributions made in taxable years beginning after December 31, 2007.
