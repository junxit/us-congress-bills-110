# S. 943

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the period for which the designation of an area as an empowerment zone is in effect.

## § 1. Extension of round II and round III empowerment zones

- **(a)** *In general* Clause (i) of section 1391(d)(1)(A) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to period for which designation is in effect) is amended by inserting “(December 31, 2016, in the case of any empowerment zone designated under subsection (g) or (h))” after “2009”.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Paragraph (2) of section 1391(h) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to additional designations permitted) is amended by striking “2009” and inserting “2016”.
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall take effect on the date of the enactment of this Act.
