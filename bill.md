# H.R. 1830

> To extend the authorities of the Andean Trade Preference Act until September 30, 2009.

## § 1. Extension of Andean Trade Preference Act

- **(a)** *Extension* Section 208(a) of the Andean Trade Preference Act (19 U.S.C. 3206(a)) is amended by striking “June 30, 2007” and inserting “September 30, 2009”.
- **(b)** *Repeal of conditional extensions* Section 208 of the Andean Trade Preference Act (19 U.S.C. 3206) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “(a) Termination.—Subject to subsection (b), no” and inserting “No”; and
  - **(2)** by striking subsection (b).

## § 2. Treatment of certain apparel articles

Section 204(b)(3)(B) of the Andean Trade Preference Act (19 U.S.C. 3206(b)(3)(B)) is amended—

- **(1)** in clause (iii)—
  - **(A)** in subclause (II)—
    - **(i)** by striking “Subject to section 208, the” and inserting “The”; and
    - **(ii)** by striking “4 succeeding 1-year periods” and inserting “6 succeeding 1-year periods”; and
  - **(B)** in subclause (III)—
    - **(i)** by striking “means 2 percent” and inserting
      > means—
      > - **(aa)** 2 percent

    - **(ii)** by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
    - **(iii)** by adding at the end the following:
      > - **(bb)** for the 1-year period beginning October 1, 2007, and the 1-year period beginning October 1, 2008, the percentage determined under item (aa) for the 1-year period beginning October 1, 2006.

- **(2)** in clause (v)(II)—
  - **(A)** by striking “Subject to section 208, during” and inserting “During”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “3 succeeding 1-year periods” and inserting “5 succeeding 1-year periods”.
