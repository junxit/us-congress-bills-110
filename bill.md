# H.R. 5571

> To extend for 5 years the program relating to waiver of the foreign country residence requirement with respect to international medical graduates.

## § 1. Extension of waiver program

Section 220(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Technical Corrections Act of 1994 (8 U.S.C. 1182 note) is amended by striking “June 1, 2008” and inserting “June 1, 2013”.
