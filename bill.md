# H.R. 3308

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 216 East Main Street in Atwood, Indiana, as the “Lance Corporal David K. Fribley Post Office”.

## § 1. Lance Corporal David K. Fribley Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 216 East Main Street in Atwood, Indiana, shall be known and designated as the “Lance Corporal David K. Fribley Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Lance Corporal David K. Fribley Post Office”.
