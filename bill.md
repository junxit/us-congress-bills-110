# H.R. 935

> To insert certain counties as part of the Appalachian Region.

## § 1. Appalachian region

Section 14102(a)(1)(K) of title 40, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “Giles” after “Franklin”;
- **(2)** by inserting “Hickman” after “Hawkins”; and
- **(3)** by inserting “Lawrence, Lewis, Lincoln,” after “Knox”.
