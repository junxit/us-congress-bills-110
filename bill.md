# H.R. 2791

> To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to provide for establishment of a unique device identification system for medical devices.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Unique Device Identification System for Medical Devices Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Unique device identification system

Section 519 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 360i) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subsection (f) as subsection (g); and
- **(2)** by inserting after subsection (e) the following:
  > - **(f)** *Unique device identification system* The Secretary shall promulgate regulations establishing a unique device identification system for medical devices requiring the labeling of devices to bear a unique identifier.
