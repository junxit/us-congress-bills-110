# H.R. 6864

> To prohibit golden parachute payments for former executives and directors of Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac.

## § 1. Prohibition on golden parachutes

Section 1318(e)(1) of the Federal Housing Enterprises Financial Safety and Soundness Act of 1992 (12 U.S.C. 4518(e)(1)), as added by section 1114 of the Federal Housing Finance Regulatory Reform Act of 2008 (Public Law 110–289), is amended by inserting before the period at the end the following: “, and shall prohibit any golden parachute payment on or after the date of enactment of this subsection with respect to any executive officer or member of the board of directors of a regulated entity”.
