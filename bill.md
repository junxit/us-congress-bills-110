# H.R. 5476

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on ethofumesate in bulk or mixtures.

## § 1. Ethofumesate in bulk or mixtures

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.05.19 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to ethofumesate in bulk or mixtures) is amended—
  - **(1)** in the article description, by striking “3808.30.15” and inserting “3808.93.15”; and
  - **(2)** in the effective period column, by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
