# S. 3

> To amend part D of title XVIII of the Social Security Act to provide for fair prescription drug prices for Medicare beneficiaries.

## § 1. Short title; sense of the Congress

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Medicare Prescription Drug Price Negotiation Act of 2007”.
- **(b)** *Sense of the Congress* It is the sense of the Congress that the Congress should enact, and the President should sign, legislation to amend part D of title XVIII of the Social Security Act to provide for fair prescription drug prices for Medicare beneficiaries.
