# S. 3623

> To authorize appropriations for the Department of Homeland Security for fiscal years 2008 and 2009, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Department of Homeland Security Authorization Act of 2008 and 2009”.

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act—

- **(1)** the term Department means the Department of Homeland Security; and
- **(2)** the term Secretary means the Secretary of Homeland Security.
