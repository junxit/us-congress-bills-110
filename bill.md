# S. 3357

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain rayon staple fibers.

## § 1. Certain rayon staple fibers

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.55.04 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2011”; and
  - **(2)** by striking the “Free” in the column 1 general rate of duty column and inserting “1.9%”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
