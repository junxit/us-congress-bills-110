# H.R. 52

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the cap on the cover over of tax on distilled spirits to Puerto Rico and the Virgin Islands.

## § 1. Repeal of cap on cover over of tax on distilled spirits to Puerto Rico and Virgin Islands

- **(a)** *In general* Section 7652 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to limitation on cover over of tax on distilled spirits) is amended by striking subsection (f) and by redesignating subsections (g) and (h) as subsections (f) and (g), respectively.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Section 7652(f) of such Code (as so redesignated) is amended by striking “subsection (f) of this section” in paragraph (1)(B) and inserting “section 5001(a)(1)”.
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall take effect on January 1, 2007.
