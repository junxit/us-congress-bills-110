# S. 1319

> To provide for the conversion of a temporary judgeship for the district of Hawaii to a permanent judgeship.

## § 1. Conversion of temporary judgeship to permanent judgeship for the district of Hawaii

- **(a)** *In general* The existing judgeship for the district of Hawaii authorized by section 203(c) of the Judicial Improvements Act of 1990 (28 U.S.C. 133 note; Public Law 101–650; 104 Stat. 5089) shall, as of the date of enactment of this Act, be authorized under section 133 of title 28, United States Code, and the incumbent in that office shall hold the office under section 133 of title 28, United States Code, as amended by this Act.
