# S. 406

> To ensure local governments have the flexibility needed to enhance decision-making regarding certain mass transit projects.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Transit System Flexibility Protection Act of 2007”.

## § 2. General authority

Section 5307(b)(1) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by striking subparagraph (E) and inserting the following:
