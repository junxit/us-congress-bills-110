# S. 180

> To provide a permanent deduction for State and local general sales taxes.

## § 1. Permanent extension of deduction of State and local general sales taxes

Subparagraph (I) of section 164(b)(5) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended by section 103 of division A of the Tax Relief and Health Care Act of 2006, is amended by striking “, and before January 1, 2008”.
