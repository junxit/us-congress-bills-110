# H.R. 2814

> To authorize the Secretary of Energy to provide loan guarantees for 100 percent of the cost of construction of new domestic nuclear power production facilities.

## § 1. Nuclear plant loan guarantees

Section 1702(c) of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 (42 U.S.C. 16512(c)) is amended by adding at the end the following: “The Secretary may guarantee a loan for 100 percent of the cost of construction of new domestic nuclear power production facilities under this title.”.
