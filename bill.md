# H.R. 976

That the House agree to the amendments of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 976) entitled “An Act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide tax relief for small businesses, and for other purposes”, with the following

*In lieu of the matter proposed to be inserted by the Senate amendment to the text of the bill, insert the following:*

*In lieu of the matter proposed to be inserted by the Senate amendment to the title of the bill, insert the following: “An Act to amend title XXI of the Social Security Act to extend and improve the Children’s Health Insurance Program, and for other purposes.”.*
