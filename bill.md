# H.R. 2298

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make geothermal heat pump systems eligible for the energy credit.

## § 1. Geothermal heat pump systems treated as energy property

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (A) of section 48(a)(3) is amended by striking “or” at the end of clause (iii), by inserting “or” at the end of clause (iv), and by adding at the end the following new clause:
  > - **(v)** equipment which uses the ground or ground water as a thermal energy source to heat a structure or as a thermal energy sink to cool a structure,

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after the date of the enactment of this Act.
