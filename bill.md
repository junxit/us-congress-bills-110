# S. 1635

> To provide for the reimbursement of wildland firefighters for the cost of professional liability insurance.

## § 1. Wildland firefighter insurance

Section 636 of division A of the Treasury, Postal Service, and General Government Appropriations Act, 1997 (5 U.S.C. prec. 5941 note; Public Law 104–208), is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (1), by striking “or”;
  - **(B)** in paragraph (2), by striking the period and inserting “; or”; and
  - **(C)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(3)** a temporary fire line supervisor.

- **(2)** in subsection (c)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (3), by striking “, and” and inserting a semicolon;
  - **(B)** in paragraph (4), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(C)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(5)** the term temporary fire line supervisor means an employee of the Department of the Interior or the Forest Service whose duties include temporary supervision to manage a wildland or managed fire, including an employee that is—
    >   - **(A)** a type 1, 2, or 3 incident commander;
    >   - **(B)** an operations section chief;
    >   - **(C)** a division group supervisor;
    >   - **(D)** a fire use manager; or
    >   - **(E)** a prescribed fire manager or burn boss.
