# H.R. 118

> To amend part D of title XVIII of the Social Security Act to authorize the Secretary of Health and Human Services to negotiate prices for part D covered drugs for Medicare beneficiaries.

## § 1. Authority to negotiate Medicare part D drug prices

Subsection (i) of section 1860D–11 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395w–111) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(i)** *Negotiation authority* The Secretary is authorized to negotiate with drug manufacturers and pharmacies prices charged to PDP sponsors and MA organizations for covered part D drugs provided under this title.
