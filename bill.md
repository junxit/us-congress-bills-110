# H.R. 415

> To amend the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act to designate segments of the Taunton River in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts as a component of the National Wild and Scenic Rivers System.

## § 1. Taunton River, Massachusetts

Section 3(a) of the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act (16 U.S.C. 1274(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:

> - **(___)** *Taunton River, Massachusetts* The segment downstream from the headwaters, from the confluence of the Town River and the Matfield River in Bridgewater to the Mount Hope Bay in the city of Fall River, Massachusetts.
