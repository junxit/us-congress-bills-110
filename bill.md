# H.R. 4154

> To increase the insurance limitations on Federal insurance for bonds issued by the designated bonding authority for Historically Black Colleges and Universities capital financing.

## § 1. Limitations on Federal insurance for bonds issued by the designated bonding authority

Section 344(a) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1066c(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “$375,000,000” and inserting “$1,100,000,000”;
- **(2)** by striking “$250,000,000” and inserting “$733,333,333”; and
- **(3)** by striking “$125,000,000” and inserting “$366,666,666”.
