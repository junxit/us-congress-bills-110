# S. 1725

> To amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974, the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, and title 5, United States Code, to improve the protection of pension benefits, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Restoring Pension Promises to Workers Act”.
