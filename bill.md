# H. RES. 1342

> Electing certain Members to certain standing committees of the House of Representatives.

That the following named Members be, and are hereby, elected to the following standing committees of the House of Representatives:

- **(1)** *Committee on Oversight and Government Reform* Ms. Speier.
- **(2)** *Committee on Science and Technology* Ms. Edwards of Maryland (to rank immediately after Ms. Richardson).
- **(3)** *Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure* Ms. Edwards of Maryland.
