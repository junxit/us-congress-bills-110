# S. 222

> To amend the Haitian Hemispheric Opportunity through Partnership Encouragement Act of 2006 to extend the date for the President to determine if Haiti meets certain requirements, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Time for Presidential determination regarding Haiti

Section 213A(d)(2) of the Caribbean Basin Economic Recovery Act, as added by section 5002(a) of the Haitian Hemispheric Opportunity through Partnership Encouragement Act of 2006 (Public Law 109–432), is amended by striking “90 days” and inserting “1 year”.
