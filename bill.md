# H.R. 4372

> To extend and modify the temporary suspension of duty on ADTP.

## § 1. ADTP

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.25.33 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to 2-Amino-5,8-dimethoxy-(1,2,4)triazolo(1,5-c)pyrimidine) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “Free” and inserting “3%”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall take effect on January 1, 2010.
