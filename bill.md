# H.R. 6225

> To amend title 38, United States Code, relating to equitable relief with respect to a State or private employer.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Injunctive Relief for Veterans Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Equity powers

Section 4323(e) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “may use” and inserting “shall use, in any case in which the court determines it is appropriate,”.
