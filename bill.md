# H.R. 5492

> To authorize the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution to construct a greenhouse facility at its museum support facility in Suitland, Maryland, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Construction of greenhouse facility

The Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution is authorized to construct a greenhouse facility at its museum support facility in Suitland, Maryland, to maintain the horticultural operations of, and preserve the orchid collection held in trust by, the Smithsonian Institution.

## § 2. Authorization of appropriations

There is authorized to be appropriated $12,000,000 to carry out this Act. Such sums shall remain available until expended.
