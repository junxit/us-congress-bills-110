# H.R. 6478

> To improve research, diagnosis, and treatment of musculoskeletal diseases, conditions, and injuries, to conduct a longitudinal study on aging, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Access to America’s Orthopedic Services Act of 2008”.
