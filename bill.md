# S. 2602

> To amend the Department of the Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2008, to terminate the authority of the Secretary of the Treasury to deduct amounts from certain States.

## § 1. Share of oil and gas leasing revenues

The matter under the heading “administrative provisions” under the heading “Minerals Management Service” of title I of the Department of the Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2008 (Subdivision F of Public Law 110–161) is amended by striking the second undesignated paragraph.
