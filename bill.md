# H.R. 3624

> To establish a comprehensive program to ensure the safety of food products intended for human consumption which are regulated by the Food and Drug Administration.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Consumer Food Safety Act of 2007”.
- **(b)** *Table of contents*

## § 2. Definitions

For purposes of this Act:

- **(1)** The term contaminants includes bacteria, chemical contaminants, natural toxins, viruses, parasites, and physical hazards that when found on or in food can cause human illness or injury.
- **(2)** The term facility includes any factory, warehouse, establishment, or importer that handles or processes food.
- **(3)** The term process means the commercial harvesting, preparation, manufacture, or transportation of food products.
- **(4)** The term Secretary means the Secretary of Health and Human Services.
