# H.R. 2192

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to establish an Ombudsman within the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Establishment of Office of the Ombudsman in Department of Veterans Affairs

- **(a)** *Establishment* Chapter 3 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 321. Office of the Ombudsman
  >
  > - **(a)** *In general* There is established in the Department an Office of the Ombudsman (in this section referred to as the Office). The Secretary shall designate an Ombudsman to carry out the duties of the Office.
  > - **(b)** *Duties* The Office shall act as a liaison for veterans and their family members with respect to the receipt of health care and benefits administered by the Department.
  > - **(c)** *Availability of services* The Secretary shall ensure that the services of the Office are available to all veterans and their family members.
  > - **(d)** *Confidentiality* Any information provided by veterans or their family members to the Office may be disclosed only to the extent necessary to carry out the duties of the Office.
  > - **(e)** *Outreach* The Secretary shall make available to each veteran, and to the family members of all veterans, information on contacting and utilizing the services of the Office.
