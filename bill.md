# H.R. 1003

> To amend the Foreign Affairs Reform and Restructuring Act of 1998 to reauthorize the United States Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy.

## § 1. Reauthorization of United States Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy

Section 1334 of the Foreign Affairs Reform and Restructuring Act of 1998 (22 U.S.C. 6553) is amended by striking “October 1, 2006” and inserting “October 1, 2009”.
