# H. RES. 532

> Recognizing the energy and economic partnership between the United States and Honduras.

## Preamble

- Whereas the energy and economic partnership between the United States and Honduras is mutually beneficial to both the citizens of the United States and Honduras;
- Whereas Honduras should be commended for seeking alternative fuel sources such as bio-diesel;
- Whereas the United States will be able to diversify its fuel supply and import energy from a democratically-elected country such as Honduras;
- Whereas the partnership between the United States and Honduras should help Honduras with the necessary resources to improve its infrastructure, such as roads and medical clinics; and
- Whereas this should be the beginning of a long and prosperous energy and economic partnership between the United States and Honduras: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** recognizes the energy and economic partnership between the United States and Honduras;
- **(2)** honors all who are working for the betterment of both countries; and
- **(3)** offers its hope that this is the beginning of a long and prosperous energy and economic partnership between the United States and Honduras.
