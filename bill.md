# H.R. 6789

> To remove the incentives and loopholes that encourage illegal aliens to come to the United States to live and work, provide additional resources to local law enforcement and Federal border and immigration officers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Loophole Elimination and Verification Enforcement Act” or the “LEAVE Act” .

## § 2. Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to prohibit all activities that assist, encourage, direct, or induce an alien to reside in or remain in the United States or to attempt to reside in the United States, knowing or in reckless disregard of the fact that the alien is not authorized to be present in the United States.
