# H. RES. 231

> Amending the Rules of the House of Representatives to require all committees post record votes on their Web sites within 48 hours of such votes.

That clause 2(e)(1)(B)(i) of rule XI of the Rules of the House of Representatives is amended—

- **(1)** in the first sentence, by inserting “and within 48 hours of such record vote on its Web site on the Internet” before the period at the end thereof; and
- **(2)** in the second sentence, by inserting “and information so available on its Web site” before “shall”.
