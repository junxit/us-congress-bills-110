# H.R. 5470

> To amend the Communications Act of 1934 to require the carriage of all local television signals by satellite carriers in all local markets.

## § 1. Satellite carriage of all local television signals in all local markets required

- **(a)** *National Local-to-local* Section 338(a)(1) of the Communications Act of 1934 (47 U.S.C. 338(a)(1)) is amended by striking “within that local market” and inserting “within any local market”.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Section 338(a) of such Act (47 U.S.C. 338(a)) is amended by striking “(3) Effective date.—” and all that follows through “2002.”.

## § 2. Effective Date

The amendments made by section 1 shall be effective on February 17, 2009.
