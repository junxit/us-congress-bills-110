# H.R. 77

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to change the deadline for income tax returns for calendar year taxpayers from the 15th of April to the first Monday in November.

## § 1. Deadline for filing income tax returns for calendar year taxpayers changed from April 15th to first Monday of November

- **(a)** *In general* Section 6072 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to time for filing income tax returns) is amended in subsection (a) by striking “15th day of April” and inserting “first Monday in November”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2007.
