# H.R. 874

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to provide an additional penalty for public officials who abuse their office in furtherance of a felony.

## § 1. Abuse of office in furtherance of a felony

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 93 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > ## § 1925. Abuse of office in furtherance of a felony.
  >
  > - **(a)** Whoever, as a public official, engages, or undertakes to engage, in any conduct in furtherance of a Federal felony, shall, in addition to the punishment provided for such felony, be fined under this title and imprisoned for 2 years.
  > - **(b)** In this section, the term public official means any of the following:
  >   - **(1)** An official occupying an elected office of the United States or of a State or local government.
  >   - **(2)** An official appointed to an office of the United States by the President with the advice and consent of the Senate.
  >   - **(3)** An official appointed to an office of a State or local government by an official elected to an office of a State or local government.
