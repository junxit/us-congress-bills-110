# H.R. 735

> To designate the Federal building under construction at 799 First Avenue in New York, New York, as the “Ronald H. Brown United States Mission to the United Nations Building”.

## § 1. Designation

The Federal building under construction at 799 First Avenue in New York, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Ronald H. Brown United States Mission to the United Nations Building”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Ronald H. Brown United States Mission to the United Nations Building”.
