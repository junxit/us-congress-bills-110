# S. 3192

> To amend the Act of August 9, 1955, to authorize the Cow Creek Band of Umpqua Tribe of Indians, the Coquille Indian Tribe, and the Confederated Tribes of the Siletz Indians of Oregon to obtain 99-year lease authority for trust land.

## § 1. Leases of restricted land

Subsection (a) of the first section of the Act of August 9, 1955 (25 U.S.C. 415(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the first sentence, by striking “That (a) any restricted” and inserting the following:
  > ## § 1. Leases of restricted land
  >
  > - **(a)** *Authorized purposes; term; approval by Secretary*
  >   - **(1)** *Authorized purposes* Any restricted

- **(2)** in the second sentence—
  - **(A)** by striking “All leases so granted shall be for a term of not to exceed twenty-five years, except leases” and inserting the following:
    > - **(2)** *Term*
    >   - **(A)** *In general* Except as provided in subparagraphs (B) and (C), each lease granted pursuant to paragraph (1) shall be for a term of not longer than 25 years.
    >   - **(B)** *Leases of certain areas* Notwithstanding subparagraph (A), leases

  - **(B)** in subparagraph (B) (as designated by subparagraph (A))—
    - **(i)** by striking “leases of the the” and inserting “leases of the”;
    - **(ii)** by inserting “land held in trust for the Cow Creek Band of Umpqua Tribe of Indians, land held in trust for the Coquille Indian Tribe, and land held in trust for the Confederated Tribes of the Siletz Indians of Oregon” after “Devils Lake Sioux Reservation,”; and
    - **(iii)** by striking “which may be for a term of not to exceed ninety-nine years, and except leases” and inserting the following:
      > - **(C)** *Leases for grazing purposes* Leases

  - **(C)** in subparagraph (C) (as designated by subparagraph (B)), by striking “which”;
- **(3)** in the third sentence, by striking “Leases for public” and inserting the following:
  > - **(3)** *Approval by Secretary*
  >   - **(A)** *In general* Leases for public

- **(4)** in the fourth sentence, by striking “Prior to approval of” and inserting the following:
  > - **(B)** *Conditions for approval* Before approving
