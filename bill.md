# S. 1411

> To amend the Clean Air Act to establish within the Environmental Protection Agency an office to measure and report on greenhouse gas emissions of Federal agencies.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Federal Government Greenhouse Gas Registry Act of 2007”.
