# H.R. 417

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to change the eligibility requirements for appointment as Secretary of Defense.

## § 1. Change in eligibility requirements for appointment as secretary of defense

Section 113(a) of title 10, United States Code, is amended in the second sentence by striking “10” and inserting “three”.
