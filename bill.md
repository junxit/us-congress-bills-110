# S. 88

> To increase the penalty for failure to comply with lobbying disclosure requirements.

## § 1. Increased penalty for failure to comply with lobbying disclosure requirements

Section 7 of the Lobbying Disclosure Act of 1995 (2 U.S.C. 1606) is amended by striking “$50,000” and inserting “$200,000”.
