# H.R. 2150

> To authorize appropriations to provide for South Pacific exchanges.

## § 1. South Pacific exchanges

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of State $1,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2008 and 2009 for South Pacific exchanges carried out under the educational and cultural exchange programs of the Department of State.
