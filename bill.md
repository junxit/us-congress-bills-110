# H.R. 6857

> To amend section 203(a) of the Clean Air Act to permit the conversion of a motor vehicle for the use of natural gas fuel, and for other purposes.

That section 203(a) of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7522(a)) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following: “No action with respect to any device or element of design referred to in paragraph (3) shall be treated as a prohibited act under that paragraph if the action is for the purpose of a conversion of a motor vehicle for the use of natural gas fuel.”.
