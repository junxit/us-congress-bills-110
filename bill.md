# H.R. 6841

> For the relief of Thomas J. Sherlock.

## § 1. Social security insured status for Thomas J. Sherlock

For purposes of section 214 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 414), Thomas J. Sherlock of Aurora, Colorado, shall be deemed to have 4 quarters of coverage for each of calendar years 1979 through 1986.
