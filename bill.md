# S. 1399

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to combine the Hope Scholarship Credit and the deduction for qualified tuition and related expenses into a refundable college affordability and creating chances for educational success for students (ACCESS) credit, to establish an Early Federal Pell Grant Commitment Demonstration Program, and to increase the maximum Federal Pell Grant Award.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “College Affordability and Creating Chances for Educational Success for Students Act of 2007”.
