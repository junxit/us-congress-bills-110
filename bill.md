# H.R. 5612

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to waive the deadline on the construction of GO Zone property which is eligible for bonus depreciation.

## § 1. Waiver of deadline on construction of GO Zone property eligible for bonus depreciation

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (B) of section 1400N(d)(3) is amended to read as follows:
  > - **(B)** without regard to “and before January 1, 2009” in clause (i) thereof,

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after December 31, 2007.
