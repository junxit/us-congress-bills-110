# H.R. 5582

> To authorize appropriations for the National Historical Publications and Records Commission through fiscal year 2014.

## § 1. Authorization of appropriations through fiscal year 2014 for national historical publications and records commission

Section 2504(f)(1) of title 44, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (R), by striking “and”;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (S), by striking the period and inserting a semicolon; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end of the following new subparagraph:
  > - **(T)** $20,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, and 2014.
