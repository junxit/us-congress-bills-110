# H.R. 2440

> To reauthorize the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “FinCEN Reauthorization Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Finding

The Congress finds that the work of the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network is essential to safeguard the United States financial system and its international affiliates from the abuses of financial crime, including terrorist financing, developing weapons of mass destruction, and money laundering.

## § 3. Reauthorization of the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network

Section 310(d)(1) of title 31, United States Code, is amended by striking “such sums as may be necessary for fiscal years 2002, 2003, 2004, and 2005” and inserting “not more than $85,844,000 for fiscal year 2008, and such sums as may be neccessary for fiscal years 2009, 2010, 2011, and 2012”.
