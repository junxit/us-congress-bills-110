# H.R. 1924

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide credit rate parity for all renewable resources under the electricity production credit.

## § 1. Credit rate parity for all renewable resources under electricity production credit

- **(a)** *In general* Section 45(b)(4)(A) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to credit rate) is amended by inserting “and before 2007” after “2003”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to electricity produced and sold after December 31, 2006.
