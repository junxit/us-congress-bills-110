# H.R. 5664

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to update at least once every six years the plans and specifications for specially adapted housing furnished to veterans by the Secretary.

## § 1. Regular update of specially adapted housing plans and specifications furnished to veterans by Secretary of Veterans Affairs

Section 2103 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new sentence: “The Secretary shall update such plans and specifications at least once every six years.”.
