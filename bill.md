# H.R. 4004

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from gross income the stipends received for working as an election judge.

## § 1. Exclusion from gross income for stipends received by election judges

- **(a)** *In general* Part III of subchapter B of chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to items specifically excluded from gross income) is amended by inserting after section 139A the following new section:
  > ## § 139B. Election judge stipends
  >
  > - **(a)** *Exclusion* Gross income shall not include any amount received by an individual from a State or local government as a stipend—
  >   - **(1)** for services performed as an election judge for any Federal, State, or local election, or
  >   - **(2)** for completing a training course to be an election judge for such an election.
  > - **(b)** *Election judge* For purposes of this section, the term election judge includes poll workers and other individuals performing similar functions.
