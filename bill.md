# H.R. 5360

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on brass lamp-holder housings containing sockets.

## § 1. Brass lamp-holder housings containing sockets

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.23.32 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to lamp-holder housings of brass, containing sockets) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
