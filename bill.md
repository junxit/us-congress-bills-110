# H.R. 1750

> To amend the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act to extend from 90 days to one year the period after release of a member of the Armed Forces from active duty during which the member is protected from mortgage foreclosure under that Act.

## § 1. Extension of period of mortgage foreclosure protection under Servicemembers Civil Relief Act

- **(a)** *Extension to 12 months* Section 303(c) of the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (50 U.S.C. App. 533(c)) is amended by striking “90 days” and inserting “one year”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to any person performing a period of military service (as such term is defined in section 101(2) of such Act (50 U.S.C. App. 511(2))) that begins on or after February 1, 2003.
