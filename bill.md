# S. 2694

> To authorize to be appropriated to the Defense Logistics Agency for fiscal year 2009 $14,400,000 to replace fuel storage tanks at Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico.

## § 1. Replacement of fuel storage tanks at Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico

- **(a)** *Project authorization* The Secretary of Defense may replace fuel storage tanks at Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico, in the amount of $14,400,000.
- **(b)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated $14,400,000 for fiscal year 2009 for military construction, land acquisition, and military family housing functions of the Department of Defense (other than the military departments) to carry out the project authorized under subsection (a).
