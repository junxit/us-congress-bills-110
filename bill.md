# H.R. 7012

> To amend title 23, United States Code, to authorize States to issue special permits to allow the operation of vehicles of not to exceed 95,000 pounds on Interstate System highways for the hauling of livestock.

## § 1. Vehicle weight limitations for Interstate System highways

Section 127(a) of title 23, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(13)** *Hauling of livestock* A State may allow, by special permit, the operation of vehicles with a gross vehicle weight of not to exceed 95,000 pounds for the hauling of livestock. The cost of a special permit issued under this paragraph may not exceed $200 per year for a livestock trailer.
