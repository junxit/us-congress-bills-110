# S. 1928

> To amend section 1977A of the Revised Statutes to equalize the remedies available under that section.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Equal Remedies Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Equalization of remedies

Section 1977A of the Revised Statutes (42 U.S.C. 1981a) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b)—
  - **(A)** by striking paragraph (3); and
  - **(B)** by redesignating paragraph (4) as paragraph (3); and
- **(2)** in subsection (c), by striking “section—” and all that follows through the period, and inserting “section, any party may demand a jury trial.”.
