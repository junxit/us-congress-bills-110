# H.R. 156

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to provide for the payment of dependency and indemnity compensation to the survivors of former prisoners of war who died on or before September 30, 1999, under the same eligibility conditions as apply to payment of dependency and indemnity compensation to the survivors of former prisoners of war who die after that date.

That section 1318(b)(3) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “who died after September 30, 1999,”.
