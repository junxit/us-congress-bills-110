# H.R. 6229

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 2523 7th Avenue East in North Saint Paul, Minnesota, as the “Mayor William “Bill” Sandberg Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Mayor William “Bill” Sandberg Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 2523 7th Avenue East in North Saint Paul, Minnesota, shall be known and designated as the “Mayor William “Bill” Sandberg Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Mayor William “Bill” Sandberg Post Office Building”.
