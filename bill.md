# S. 872

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the excise tax provisions and income tax credit for biodiesel.

## § 1. Extension of excise tax provisions and income tax credit for biodiesel

- **(a)** *In general* Sections 40A(g), 6426(c)(6), and 6427(e)(5)(B) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 are each amended by striking “2008” and inserting “2017”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall take effect on the date of the enactment of this Act.
