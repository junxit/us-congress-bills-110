# H. RES. 1025

> Providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1843) to extend the termination date for the exemption of returning workers from numerical limitations for temporary workers.

That upon the adoption of this resolution the House shall immediately proceed to consider in the House the bill (H.R. 1843) to extend the termination date for the exemption of returning workers from numerical limitations for temporary workers. All points of order against the bill and against its consideration are waived. The bill shall be considered as read. The previous question shall be considered as ordered on the bill to final passage without intervening motion except: (1) one hour of debate equally divided and controlled by the chairman and ranking minority member of the Committee on the Judiciary; and (2) one motion to recommit.
