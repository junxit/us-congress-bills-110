# H.R. 3596

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent law the tax-free distributions from individual retirement plans for charitable purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Charitable Tax Relief Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Tax-free distributions from individual retirement plans for charitable purposes made permanent law

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (8) of section 408(d) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking subparagraph (F).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this subsection shall apply to distributions made taxable years beginning after December 31, 2007.
