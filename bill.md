# H.R. 3312

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the work opportunity tax credit for Hurricane Katrina employees.

## § 1. Extension of work opportunity tax credit for Hurricane Katrina employees

- **(a)** *In general* Section 201(b)(1) of the Katrina Emergency Tax Relief Act of 2005 is amended by striking “2-year” and inserting “4-year”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect on the date of the enactment of this Act.
