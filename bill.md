# H.R. 6477

> To repeal a limitation in the Labor-Management Relations Act regarding requirements for labor organization membership as a condition of employment.

## § 1. Repeal

Section 14 of the Labor-Management Relations Act (29 U.S.C. 164) is amended by striking subsection (b) and redesignating subsection (c) as subsection (b).
