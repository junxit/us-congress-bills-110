# S. 1995

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to reduce the tax on beer to its pre-1991 level.

## § 1. Repeal of 1990 tax increase on beer

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (1) of section 5051(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to imposition and rate of tax on beer) is amended by striking “$18” and inserting “$9”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to beer removed for consumption or sale, or beer imported into the United States, after the date of the enactment of this Act.
