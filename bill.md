# S. 2622

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 11001 Dunklin Road in St. Louis, Missouri, as the “William “Bill” Clay Post Office”.

## § 1. William “Bill” Clay Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 11001 Dunklin Road in St. Louis, Missouri, shall be known and designated as the “William “Bill” Clay Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “William “Bill” Clay Post Office”.
