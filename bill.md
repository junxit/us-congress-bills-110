# S. 1391

> To amend the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 to authorize the Secretary of Education to award grants for the support of full-service community schools, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Full-Service Community Schools Act of 2007”.
