# H.R. 430

> To designate the United States bankruptcy courthouse located at 271 Cadman Plaza East in Brooklyn, New York, as the “Conrad B. Duberstein United States Bankruptcy Courthouse”.

## § 1. Designation

The United States bankruptcy courthouse located at 271 Cadman Plaza East in Brooklyn, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Conrad B. Duberstein United States Bankruptcy Courthouse”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the United States bankruptcy courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Conrad B. Duberstein United States Bankruptcy Courthouse”.
