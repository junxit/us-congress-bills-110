# H.R. 2444

> To amend title 4, United States Code, to provide that it is especially appropriate to display the flag on Father’s Day.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Honoring America’s Fathers Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Finding of Congress

Upon the recommendation of the students of Roosevelt Middle School in Coffeyville, Kansas, the Congress finds that it is appropriate to recognize fathers by displaying the flag on Father’s Day.

## § 3. Display of flag on Father’s Day

Section 6(d) of title 4, United States Code, is amended by inserting “Father’s Day, third Sunday in June;” after “Flag Day, June 14;”.
