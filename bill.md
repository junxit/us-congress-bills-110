# H.R. 465

> To reauthorize the Asian Elephant Conservation Act of 1997.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Asian Elephant Conservation Reauthorization Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of African Elephant Conservation Act

Section 8(a) of the Asian Elephant Conservation Act of 1997 (16 U.S.C. 4266(a)) is amended by striking “2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005, 2006, and 2007” and inserting “2007 through 2012”.
