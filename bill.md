# H.R. 7023

> To redesignate the third powerhouse facility administered by the Bureau of Reclamation and located at Grand Coulee Dam in Grand Coulee, Washington, as the “John W. Keys, III Powerplant”, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Redesignation of facility

The facility administered by the Bureau of Reclamation, located at Grand Coulee Dam in Grand Coulee, Washington, and known as the third powerhouse, shall be known and designated as the “John W. Keys, III Powerplant”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “John W. Keys, III Powerplant”.
