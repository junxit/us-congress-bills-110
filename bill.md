# H.R. 2709

> To extend the minimum wage index established by regulation for each all-urban State under the Medicare inpatient hospital prospective payment system.

## § 1. Extension of the minimum wage index established by regulation for each all-urban State under the Medicare inpatient hospital prospective payment system

Section 412.64 of title 42 of the Code of Federal Regulations shall be administered as if “and before September 30, 2007” were deleted from paragraph (h)(4) of such section.
