# H.R. 1064

> To amend title 39, United States Code, to extend for 2 years the provisions under which the special postage stamp for breast cancer research is issued.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Breast Cancer Research Stamp Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Breast cancer stamp extension

Section 414(h) of title 39, United States Code, is amended by striking “2007” and inserting “2009”.
