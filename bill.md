# S. 782

> To designate the United States courthouse to be constructed in Jackson, Mississippi, as the “Thad Cochran United States Courthouse”.

## § 1. Designation

The United States courthouse to be constructed at the site bounded on the north by Court Street, on the west by West Street, on the south by South Street, and on the east by President Street in Jackson, Mississippi, shall be known and designated as the “Thad Cochran United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Thad Cochran United States Courthouse”.
