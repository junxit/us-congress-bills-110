# H.R. 693

> To amend title 40, United States Code, to require restroom gender parity in Federal buildings.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Restroom Gender Parity in Federal Buildings Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Restroom gender parity in Federal buildings

- **(a)** *In general* Section 3307(b) of title 40, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in paragraph (5) by striking “and” at the end;
  - **(2)** in paragraph (6) by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(7)** with respect to the facility to be constructed, altered, or acquired, or the space to be leased, a statement by the Administrator that the number of toilets in women’s restrooms will exceed the number of toilets (including urinals) in men’s restrooms by a ratio of 2 to 1 or a statement by the Administrator as to why such a ratio of toilets is not needed.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall become effective on the first day of the first fiscal year beginning after the date of enactment of this Act.
