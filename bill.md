# H.R. 2615

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to repeal the limitation on the authority of the Department of Defense to issue security clearances for certain persons.

## § 1. Repeal of limitation on authority of Department of Defense to issue security clearances

- **(a)** *Repeal* Section 986 of title 10, United States Code, is repealed.
- **(b)** *Table of sections* The table of sections at the beginning of chapter 49 of such title is amended by striking the item relating to section 986.
