# S. 1671

> To reauthorize and improve the entrepreneurial development programs of the Small Business Administration, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Entrepreneurial Development Act of 2007”.

## § 3. Definitions

In this Act—

- **(1)** the terms Administration and Administrator mean the Small Business Administration and the Administrator thereof, respectively; and
- **(2)** the term small business concern has the meaning given that term in section 3 of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 632).
