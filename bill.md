## § 2. Report on third-party payment collections

- **(a)** *Study* The Secretary of Health and Human Services shall conduct a thorough study of the system of third-party payment collections for items and services furnished through the Indian Health Service.
- **(b)** *Report* Not later than 6 months after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary shall submit to each House of Congress a report on such study. Such report shall include such recommendations on how to improve such third-party payment collections as the Secretary determines appropriate.
