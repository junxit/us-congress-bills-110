# H.R. 7236

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to increase the deduction for business start-up expenditures from $5,000 to $10,000.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Business Start-Up Support Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Increased deduction for business start-up expenditures

- **(a)** *In general* Clause (ii) of section 195(b)(1)(A) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to start-up expenditures) is amended by striking “$5,000” and inserting “$10,000”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to active trades or businesses beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act in taxable years ending after such date.
