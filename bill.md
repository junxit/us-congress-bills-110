# H.R. 384

> To include Nelson County, Virginia, in the Appalachian region for purposes of the programs of the Appalachian Regional Commission.

## § 1. Inclusion of Nelson county and Franklin county, Virginia, in the Appalachian region

Section 14102(a)(1)(L) of title 40, United States Code, is amended by inserting “Nelson,” after “Montgomery,”.
