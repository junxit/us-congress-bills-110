# H.R. 3566

> To permanently extend the waiver authority of the Secretary under the Higher Education Relief Opportunities for Students Act of 2003.

## § 1. Permanent extension

Section 6 of the Higher Education Relief Opportunities for Students Act of 2003 (Public Law 108–76; 20 U.S.C. 1070, note) is repealed.
