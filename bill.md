# H.R. 6862

> To reauthorize the Marine Turtle Conservation Act of 2004.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Marine Turtle Conservation Reauthorization Act of 2008 ”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of the Marine Turtle Conservation Act of 2004

Section 7 of the Marine Turtle Conservation Act of 2004 (Public Law 108–266; 16 U.S.C. 6606) is amended by striking “each of fiscal years 2005 through 2009” and inserting “each of fiscal years 2008 through 2014”.
