# H.R. 4373

> To extend and modify the temporary suspension of duty on Cyhalofop.

## § 1. Cyhalofop

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.02.86 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Propanoic acid, 2-[4-(cyano-2-fluorophenoxy)phenoxy]-butyl ester (2R)) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “1.5%” and inserting “2.0%”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall take effect on January 1, 2010.
