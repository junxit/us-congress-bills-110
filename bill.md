# S. 2587

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to provide for compensation to States incarcerating undocumented aliens charged with a felony or 2 or more misdemeanors.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “SCAAP Reimbursement Protection Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Assistance for States incarcerating undocumented aliens charged with certain crimes

Section 241(i)(3)(A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1231(i)(3)(A)) is amended by inserting “charged with or” before “convicted”.
