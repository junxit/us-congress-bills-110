# H.R. 4375

> To extend and modify the temporary suspension of duty on 2-Cyanopyridine.

## § 1. 2-Cyanopyridine

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.22.35 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to 2-Cyanopyridine) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “Free” and inserting “3.2%”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall take effect on January 1, 2010.
