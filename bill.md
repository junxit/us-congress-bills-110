# S. 1559

> To amend the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 to reauthorize the provision of telemedicine and distance learning services in rural areas.

## § 1. Telemedicine and distance learning services in rural areas

- **(a)** *In general* Section 2335A of the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 (7 U.S.C. 950aaa–5) is amended by striking “2007” and inserting “2012”.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Section 1(b) of Public Law 102–551 (7 U.S.C. 950aaa note) is amended by striking “2007” and inserting “2012”.
