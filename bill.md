# H.R. 3092

> For the relief of Kimberly Ruth.

## § 1. Certificate of citizenship for Kimberly Ruth

The Secretary of Homeland Security shall issue to Kimberly Ruth a new Certificate of Citizenship reflecting her actual birth date of August 3, 1966.
