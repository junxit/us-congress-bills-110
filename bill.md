# H.R. 2624

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to provide eligibility to the District of Columbia for the State Loan Repayment Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “District of Columbia Loan Repayment Equity Technical Amendment of 2007”.

## § 2. Eligibility of the District of Columbia

Section 338I(h) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254q–1(h)) is amended by striking the period and inserting “and the District of Columbia.”.
