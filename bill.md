# S. 1961

> To expand the boundaries of the Little River Canyon National Preserve in the State of Alabama.

## § 1. Little River Canyon National Preserve Boundary Expansion

Section 2 of the Little River Canyon National Preserve Act of 1992 (16 U.S.C. 698q) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b)—
  - **(A)** by striking “The Preserve” and inserting the following:
    > - **(1)** *In general* The Preserve

  - **(B)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(2)** *Boundary expansion* The boundary of the Preserve is modified to include the land depicted on the map entitled “Little River Canyon National Preserve Proposed Boundary”, numbered 152/80,004, and dated July 31, 2007December 2007.

- **(2)** in subsection (c), by striking “map” and inserting “maps”.
