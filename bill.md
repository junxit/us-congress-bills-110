# H.R. 4787

> To suspend temporarily the duty on Flonicamid.

## § 1. Flonicamid

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by striking heading 9902.24.57 (relating to Flonicamid) and inserting in numerical sequence the following new heading:
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
