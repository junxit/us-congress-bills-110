# H.R. 5454

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to establish a presumption of service connection of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis for purposes of the laws administered by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Presumption of service connection for amyotrophic lateral sclerosis

Section 1112(a) is amended by inserting after paragraph (5) the following new paragraph:

> - **(6)** amyotrophic lateral sclerosis developing a 10 percent degree of disability or more at any time;
