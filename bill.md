# H.R. 4203

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 3035 Stone Mountain Street in Lithonia, Georgia, as the “Specialist Jamaal RaShard Addison Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Specialist Jamaal RaShard Addison Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 3035 Stone Mountain Street in Lithonia, Georgia, shall be known and designated as the “Specialist Jamaal RaShard Addison Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Specialist Jamaal RaShard Addison Post Office Building”.
