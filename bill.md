# H.R. 2084

> To reform Federal budget procedures, to impose spending safeguards, to combat waste, fraud, and abuse, to account for accurate Government agency costs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short Title* This Act may be cited as the “Family Budget Protection Act of 2007”.
- **(b)** *Table of Contents*

## § 2. Effective date

Except as otherwise specifically provided, this Act and the amendments made by this Act shall become effective on the date of enactment of this Act and shall apply with respect to fiscal years beginning after September 30, 2008.
