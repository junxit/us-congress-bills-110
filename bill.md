# S. 172

> To prohibit Federal funding for the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Exit the OECD Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Prohibition of funding for the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no Federal funds may be expended to fund activities or projects undertaken by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development.
