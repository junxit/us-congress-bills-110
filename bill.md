# H.R. 1623

> To improve graduation rates by authorizing the Secretary of Education to make grants to improve adolescent literacy, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Graduation for All Act”.

## § 2. Findings

The Congress finds as follows:

- **(1)** Failure to earn a high school diploma has serious economic and social consequences for individuals and the Nation.
- **(2)** Recent studies show that less than 75 percent of all eighth graders graduate from high school in 5 years, and in urban schools the rate falls below 50 percent.
- **(3)** 40 percent of students attending high-minority-enrollment secondary schools enroll in remedial coursework when entering higher education in an effort to gain the skills their secondary education failed to provide.
- **(4)** Students who receive concentrated, focused support in literacy graduate from high school and attend college in far greater numbers.
- **(5)** On the 2002 National Assessment of Educational Progress, one in four students in the eighth grade scored below basic in reading.
- **(6)** Improving the literacy skills of adolescents is a key factor in improving high school graduation rates.
