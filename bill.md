# H.R. 2474

> To provide for an increased maximum civil penalty for violations under the Consumer Product Safety Act.

## § 1. Civil Penalties of the Consumer Product Safety Commission

Section 20(a)(1) of the Consumer Product Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 2069(a)(1)) is amended by striking “$1,250,000” each place it appears and inserting “$20,000,000”.
