# S. 1138

> To enhance nuclear safeguards and to provide assurances of nuclear fuel supply to countries that forgo certain fuel cycle activities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Nuclear Safeguards and Supply Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Table of contents

## § 3. Appropriate congressional committees defined

In this Act, the term appropriate congressional committees means the Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate and the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives.
