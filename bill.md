# H. RES. 904

> Commending the Northport American Legion Post 694 located in Northport, New York, for raising funds for the Marine and Army combat units fighting in the Middle East, enabling them to purchase needed equipment.

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** commends the Northport American Legion Post 694 located in Northport, New York, for raising more than $75,000 for the Marine and Army combat units fighting in the Middle East, enabling them to purchase welding equipment, ballistic combat goggles, thigh-rig drop pouches, special rifle slings, combat gloves, and other needed equipment; and
- **(2)** commends other American Legion Posts nationwide that have raised funds for similar purposes.
