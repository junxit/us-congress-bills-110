> To ensure the implementation of the recommendations of the National Commission on Terrorist Attacks Upon the United States.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short Title* This Act may be cited as the “Ensuring Implementation of the 9/11 Commission Report Act”.

In this Act, the term “9/11 Commission” means the National Commission on Terrorist Attacks Upon the United States.
