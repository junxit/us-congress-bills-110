# H.R. 6342

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 440 2nd Avenue in Gallipolis, Ohio, as the “Bob Evans Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Bob Evans Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 440 2nd Avenue in Gallipolis, Ohio, shall be known and designated as the “Bob Evans Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Bob Evans Post Office Building”.
