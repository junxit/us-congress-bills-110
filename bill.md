# H.R. 1027

> To amend title 36, United States Code, to authorize the spouse and siblings of a recipient of the Purple Heart medal to become associate members in the Military Order of the Purple Heart of the United States of America, Incorporated.

## § 1. Persons eligible for associate membership in the Military Order of the Purple Heart

Section 140503(b) of title 36, United States Code, is amended by striking “parents and” and inserting “the parents, spouse, siblings, and”.
