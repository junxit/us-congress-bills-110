# H. CON. RES. 135

> Recognizing Pennsylvania hunters for their continued commitment to safety and for setting a new State safety record in 2006.

## Preamble

- Whereas in 2006 Pennsylvania hunters set a new State safety record;
- Whereas Pennsylvania hunters continue year after year to improve their safety record;
- Whereas much of the success of this safety record can be contributed to hunter education training and the role of responsible hunters in the field;
- Whereas volunteer hunter education instructors have also greatly contributed to the safety of hunters; and
- Whereas hunters are the vital link in preserving and maintaining the great natural resources in Pennsylvania, including wild places: Now, therefore, be it

That the Congress recognizes Pennsylvania hunters for their continued commitment to safety and for setting a new State safety record in 2006.
