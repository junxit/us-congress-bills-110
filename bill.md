# H. RES. 1269

> Honoring the 110th anniversary of the Declaration of Independence of the Philippines.

That Congress—

- **(1)** recognizes the historic significance of the 110th anniversary of the establishment of the sovereign and independent country of the Philippines on June 12, 1898;
- **(2)** reaffirms the bonds of friendship and cooperation which have existed between the United States and the Philippines and commits to strengthening those bonds;
- **(3)** commends the people of the Philippines for enduring through struggle and hardship;
- **(4)** reaffirms its enduring support for the Philippines;
- **(5)** supports increased public awareness of the events surrounding the Declaration of Independence of the Philippines, and the vibrant and beautiful culture of the Philippines; and
- **(6)** recognizes the courage and bravery of the Filipino and Filipino American servicemen and servicewomen who have fought alongside and in the United States Armed Forces.
