# S. 2779

> To amend the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977 to clarify that uncertified States and Indian tribes have the authority to use certain payments for certain noncoal reclamation projects.

## § 1. Abandoned mine reclamation

- **(a)** *Limitation on funds* Section 409(b) of the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977 (30 U.S.C. 1239(b)) is amended by inserting “or section 411(h)(1)” after “section 402(g)”.
- **(b)** *Use of funds* Section 411(h)(1)(D)(ii) of the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977 (30 U.S.C. 1240a(h)(1)(D)(ii)) is amended by inserting “or 409” after “section 403”.
