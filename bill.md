# S. 1788

> To provide for the mandatory revocation of passports of individuals whose child support payments are more than $2,500 in arrears.

## § 1. Mandatory revocation of passports of individuals whose child support payments are more than $2,500 in arrears

Section 452(k) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 652(k)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1), by striking “, revocation, or limitation” and inserting “or revocation”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (2), by striking “may revoke, restrict, or limit” and inserting “shall revoke”.
