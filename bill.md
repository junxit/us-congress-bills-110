# S. 176

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to permanently extend the Indian employment credit and the depreciation rules for property used predominantly within an Indian reservation.

## § 1. Permanent extension of Indian employment credit and depreciation rules for property on Indian reservations

- **(a)** *Employment credit*
  - **(1)** *In general* Section 45A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to Indian employment credit) is amended by striking subsection (f).
  - **(2)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this subsection shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2007.
- **(b)** *Depreciation rules*
  - **(1)** *In general* Subsection (j) of section 168 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to property on Indian reservations) is amended by striking paragraph (8).
  - **(2)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this subsection shall apply to property placed in service after December 31, 2007.
