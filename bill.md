# S. 2985

> To amend the Safe, Accountable, Flexible, Efficient Transportation Equity Act: A Legacy for Users to correct a reference relating to a transit project in Orleans Parish, Louisiana.

## § 1. Project modification, Orleans Parish, Louisiana

The description for item 67 in section 3044(a) of the Safe, Accountable, Flexible, Efficient Transportation Equity Act: A Legacy for Users (Public Law 109–59) is amended to read as follows: “Union Passenger Terminal Planning and Master Plan and Infrastructure Improvements in Orleans Parish, Louisiana”.
