# H.R. 5133

> To increase funding for the program of block grants to States for social services.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Aiding Social Services in States Temporarily Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Increase in funding for the social services block grant program

Section 2003(c) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1397b(c)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (10), by striking “and”;
- **(2)** in paragraph (11), by striking the period and inserting “other than fiscal year 2008; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(12)** $3,700,000,000 for the fiscal year 2008.
