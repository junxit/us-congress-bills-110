# S. 298

> To provide incentives for renewable energy production, to increase fuel economy standards for automobiles, and to provide tax incentives for renewable energy production.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Renewable Energy, Fuel Reduction, and Economic Stabilization and EnHancement Act of 2007” or the “REFRESH Act”.
