# H.R. 5590

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to permit physical therapy services to be furnished under the Medicare Program to individuals under the care of a dentist.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Medicare Oral Health Rehabilitative Enhancement Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Permitting physical therapy to be furnished under the Medicare program under the care of a dentist

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1861(p)(1) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395w(p)(1)) is amended by inserting “(2),” after “(1),”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to items and services furnished on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.
