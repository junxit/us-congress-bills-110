# H.R. 5060

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to allow athletes admitted as nonimmigrants described in section 101(a)(15)(P) of such Act to renew their period of authorized admission in 5-year increments.

## § 1. Period of authorized admission for certain nonimmigrant athletes

Section 214(a)(2)(B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1184(a)(2)(B)) is amended by striking “for an additional period of up to 5 years.” and inserting “in 5-year increments.”.
