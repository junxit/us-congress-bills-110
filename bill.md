# H.R. 3468

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1704 Weeksville Road in Elizabeth City, North Carolina, as the “Dr. Clifford Bell Jones, Sr. Post Office”.

## § 1. Dr. Clifford Bell Jones, Sr. Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1704 Weeksville Road in Elizabeth City, North Carolina, shall be known and designated as the “Dr. Clifford Bell Jones, Sr. Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Dr. Clifford Bell Jones, Sr. Post Office”.
