# S. 110

> To allow the psychiatric or psychological examinations required under chapter 313 of title 18, United States Code, relating to offenders with mental disease or defect, to be conducted by a clinical social worker.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Psychiatric and Psychological Examinations Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Examinations by clinical social workers

Section 4247(b) of title 18, United States Code, is amended, in the first sentence, by striking “psychiatrist or psychologist” and inserting “psychiatrist, psychologist, or clinical social worker”.
