# S. RES. 233

> Making minority party appointments for the Select Committee on Ethics for the 110th Congress.

That the following be the minority membership on the Select Committee on Ethics for the remainder of the 110th Congress, or until their successors are appointed:

-  Mr. Cornyn, Mr. Roberts, and Mr. Isakson.
