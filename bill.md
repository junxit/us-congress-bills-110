# H.R. 231

> To authorize an additional district judgeship for the district of Nebraska.

## § 1. District judgeship for the district of Nebraska

- **(a)** *In general* The President shall appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, 1 additional district judge for the district of Nebraska.
