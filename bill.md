# S. CON. RES. 68

## § 1. Use of the rotunda of the Capitol by the Joint Congressional Committee on Inaugural Ceremonies

The rotunda of the United States Capitol is authorized to be used on January 20, 2009, by the Joint Congressional Committee on Inaugural Ceremonies in connection with the proceedings and ceremonies conducted for the inauguration of the President-elect and the Vice President-elect of the United States.
