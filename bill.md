# H.R. 4811

> To extend the suspension of duty on 2,3-quinoline dicarboxylic acid.

## § 1. 2,3-quinoline dicarboxylic acid

Heading 9902.24.19 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to 2,3-quinoline dicarboxylic acid) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2011”.
