# H.R. 2492

> To protect the welfare of consumers by prohibiting price gouging with respect to road transportation fuel or domestic heating fuel during certain abnormal market disruptions.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “The Motorist’s Bill of Rights: Increasing Gas Supply, Reducing Demand, and Protecting Consumers from Gouging”.
