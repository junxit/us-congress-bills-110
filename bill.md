# H. RES. 1350

> Providing for consideration of motions to suspend the rules.

That it shall be in order at any time on the legislative day of Thursday, July 17, 2008, for the Speaker to entertain motions that the House suspend the rules relating to a measure concerning the domestic production of oil and natural gas.
