# H.R. 615

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to increase inpatient hospital payments under the Medicare Program to Puerto Rico hospitals.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Puerto Rico Medicare Reimbursement Equity Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Modification of Medicare inpatient hospital payment rate for Puerto Rico hospitals

Section 1886(d)(9)(E) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395ww(d)(9)(E)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “and” at the end of clause (iii);
- **(2)** in clause (iv), by inserting “and before April 1, 2007,” after “2004,” and by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following new clause:
  > - **(v)** on or after April 1, 2007, the applicable Puerto Rico percentage is 0 percent and the applicable Federal percentage is 100 percent.
