# H.R. 3527

> To extend for two months the authorities of the Overseas Private Investment Corporation.

## § 1. Temporary extension of OPIC programs

Section 235(a)(2) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 (22 U.S.C. 2195(a)(2)) is amended by striking “September 30, 2007” and inserting “November 30, 2007”.
