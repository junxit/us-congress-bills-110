# H.R. 1143

> To authorize the Secretary of the Interior to lease certain lands in Virgin Islands National Park, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Lease authorized

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, after the retained use estate at Virgin Islands National Park on the date of the enactment of this Act has been terminated and the improvements on such lands have been donated to the United States, the Secretary of the Interior may enter into a lease with the person holding the retained use estate on the date of the enactment of this Act for the use of such lands and improvements on terms and conditions determined by the Secretary to be reasonable.
