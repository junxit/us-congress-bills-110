# H.R. 4393

> To extend and modify the temporary suspension of duty on Methoxyfenozide.

## § 1. Methoxyfenozide

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.32.93 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “1.0%” and inserting “4.3%”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after January 1, 2010.
