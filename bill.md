# H.R. 7123

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to increase the limitation on the capital loss carryovers of individuals to $20,000.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Middle Class Investor Relief Act”.

## § 2. Limitation on capital loss carryovers of individuals increased to $20,000

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (1) of section 1211(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to limitation on capital losses) is amended to read as follows:
  > - **(1)** $20,000 ($10,000 in the case of a married individual filing a separate return), or

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to capital losses arising in taxable years ending after the date of the enactment of this Act.
