# H.R. 4891

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to extend for an additional 5 years the special immigrant religious worker program.

## § 1. Extension of special immigrant religious worker program

Section 101(a)(27)(C)(ii) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(27)(C)(ii)) is amended by striking “2008,” each place it appears and inserting “2013,”.

## § 2. Effective date

The amendment made by section 1 shall take effect on October 1, 2008.
