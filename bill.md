# H.R. 5158

> To direct the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP Code for Windsor Heights, Iowa.

## § 1. Single, Unique ZIP Code for Windsor Heights, Iowa

Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP Code for Windsor Heights, Iowa, that encompasses the entire city.
