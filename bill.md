# H.R. 13

> To direct the Secretary of the Army to conduct a study to determine the feasibility of carrying out a project for water supply for Rialto, Fontana, and Colton, California.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Inland Empire Water Resources Preservation Act”.

## § 2. Rialto, Fontana, and Colton, California

The Secretary of the Army shall conduct a study to determine the feasibility of carrying out a project for water supply for Rialto, Fontana, and Colton, California.
