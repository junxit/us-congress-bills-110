# H.R. 3495

> To establish a National Commission on Children and Disasters, a National Resource Center on Children and Disasters, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Kids in Disasters Well-being, Safety, and Health Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Definition

In this Act, the terms child and children mean an individual or individuals, respectively, who have not attained 18 years of age.
