# H.R. 3101

> To amend the Biomass Research and Development Act of 2000 to include heating fuel produced from biomass in the definition of biobased fuel.

## § 1. Biobased fuel definition

Section 303(2) of the Biomass Research and Development Act of 2000 (7 U.S.C. 8602(2)) is amended by inserting “or heating” after “transportation”.
