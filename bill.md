# H.R. 750

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to comprehensively reform immigration law, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; references to Act

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Save America Comprehensive Immigration Act of 2007”.
- **(b)** *References to the Immigration and Nationality Act* Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Immigration and Nationality Act.
