# H.R. 918

> To provide for the construction, operation, and maintenance of a connector road in Lemay, Missouri.

## § 1. Applicability of certain Federal law

For the purpose of constructing, operating, and maintaining a connector road, known as the Lemay Connector Road (St. Louis County Arterial Road 1151), in the city and county of St. Louis, Missouri, no provision of Federal law that requires a deed restriction shall apply to parcels of real property depicted on a map dated June 1, 2006, that is on file with the St. Louis County department of highways and traffic and available for public inspection.
