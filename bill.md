# H.R. 4678

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on N-Cyclohexylthiophthalimide.

## § 1. N-Cyclohexylthiophthalimide

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking heading 9902.03.30 (relating to N-Cyclohexylthiophthalimide); and
  - **(2)** in heading 9902.22.26 (relating to N-Cyclohexylthiophthalimide), by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a)(2) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
