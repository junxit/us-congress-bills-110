# H.R. 1425

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 4551 East 52nd Street in Odessa, Texas, as the “Staff Sergeant Marvin ‘Rex’ Young Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Staff Sergeant Marvin “Rex” Young Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 4551 East 52nd Street in Odessa, Texas, shall be known and designated as the “Staff Sergeant Marvin ‘Rex’ Young Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Staff Sergeant Marvin ‘Rex’ Young Post Office Building”.
