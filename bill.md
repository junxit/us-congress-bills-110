# S. 852

> To deauthorize the project for navigation, Tenants Harbor, Maine.

## § 1. Tenants harbor, Maine

The project for navigation, Tenants Harbor, Maine, authorized by the first section of the Act of March 2, 1919 (40 Stat. 1275, chapter 95), is not authorized after the date of enactment of this Act.
