# H.R. 3353

> For the relief of Terrence George.

## § 1. Waiver of grounds for removal of, or denial of admission to, Terrence George

- **(a)** *In General* Notwithstanding sections 212(a) and 237(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Terrence George may not be removed from the United States, denied admission to the United States, denied adjustment of status to that of an alien lawfully admitted for permanent residence, or denied a nonimmigrant or immigrant visa by reason of any act of his that is a ground for removal or denial of admission and is reflected in the records of the Department of Homeland Security, or the Visa Office of the Department of State, on the date of the enactment of this Act.
- **(b)** *Establishment of Good Moral Character* Notwithstanding section 101(f) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, any act described in subsection (a) may not be considered in determining whether Terrence George is, or during any period has been, a person of good moral character for purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act.
