# H. RES. 905

> Commending the Valdosta State University Blazers on winning the NCAA Division II National Championship.

## Preamble

- Whereas on Saturday, December 15, 2007, the Valdosta State Blazers defeated Northwest Missouri State 25 to 20 with a fourth quarter comeback;
- Whereas Valdosta State became the eighth school to win more than one Division II Championship;
- Whereas Michael Terry’s one-yard game-winning touchdown run with 22 seconds left was the third latest game-winning score in regulation play in the 35-year Division II Championship history;
- Whereas the Valdosta State Blazers hold a 13 and 1 season record;
- Whereas the Valdosta State won the Division II championship in 2004, making this the second Division II championships in the last 4 years for the Blazers;
- Whereas the Blazers’ head coach, David Dean, became the second first-year head coach to lead his squad to the Division II Championship;
- Whereas the Valdosta State Blazers play with tremendous heart, talent, and sportsmanship; and
- Whereas Valdosta State University continues to build its football legacy along with engaging academics: Now, therefore, be it

That the United States House of Representatives commends the Valdosta State University Blazers for winning the NCAA Division II National Championship.
