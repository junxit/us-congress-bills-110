# H.R. 414

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 60 Calle McKinley, West in Mayaguez, Puerto Rico, as the “Miguel Angel García Méndez Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Miguel Angel García Méndez Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 60 Calle McKinley, West in Mayaguez, Puerto Rico, shall be known and designated as the “Miguel Angel García Méndez Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Miguel Angel García Méndez Post Office Building”.
