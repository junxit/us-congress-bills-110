# H.R. 973

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow physicians a credit against income tax for providing charity care.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Charity Care for the Uninsured Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Charity care credit

- **(a)** *In general* Subpart A of part IV of subchapter A of chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to nonrefundable personal credits) is amended by inserting after section 25D the following new section:

  > - **(b)** *Qualified hours of charity care* For purposes of this section—
  >   - **(1)** *Qualified hours of charity care* The term qualified hours of charity care means the hours that a physician provides medical care (as defined in section 213(d)(1)(A)) on a volunteer or pro bono basis.
  >   - **(2)** *Physician* The term physician has the meaning given to such term in section 1861(r) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395x(r)).
