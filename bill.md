# S. 2315

> To prohibit an entity under the jurisdiction of a Federal agency from paying for travel by employees of that agency.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Restoring Trust in Regulator Travel Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition

A Federal agency and any employee of that agency may not accept funding for travel from a nonfederal entity if the nonfederal entity has been subject to the jurisdiction of that agency within 2 years of the travel.
