# S. 3726

> To reauthorize the Crime-Free Rural States Grants program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Crime-Free Rural States Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Crime-Free Rural States Grants

Section 2989 of Part GG of Title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 3797y–4) is amended by striking “2003, 2004 and 2005” and inserting “2009, 2010, 2011, and 2012”.
