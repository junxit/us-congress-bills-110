# H.R. 58

> To amend the Revised Organic Act of the Virgin Islands to authorize the legislature of the Virgin Islands to create municipal governments.

## § 1. Authorization for local governments in virgin islands

Section 8 of the Revised Organic Act of the Virgin Islands (48 U.S.C. 1574) is amended by adding after subsection (c) the following new subsection:

> - **(d)** Notwithstanding section 16(a) or any other provision of law, the Legislature of the Virgin Islands may create units of local government in the Virgin Islands.
