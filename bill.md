# S. 404

> To amend the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 to require the implementation of country of origin labeling requirements by September 30, 2007.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Country of Origin Labeling Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Applicability of country of origin labeling requirements

Section 285 of the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 (7 U.S.C. 1638d) is amended by striking “September 30, 2008” and inserting “September 30, 2007”.
