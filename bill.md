# S. 2691

> To amend the Federal Crop Insurance Reform and Department of Agriculture Reorganization Act of 1994 to provide enhanced agricultural input into Federal rulemakings, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Farmer Red Tape Reduction Act of 2008”.
