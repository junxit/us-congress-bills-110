# S. 2600

> To provide for the designation of a single ZIP code for Windsor Heights, Iowa.

## § 1. Designation of single ZIP code for Windsor Heights, Iowa

Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single ZIP code applicable to the area encompassing only Windsor Heights, Iowa.
