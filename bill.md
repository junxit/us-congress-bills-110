# S. 1888

> To amend title 4, United States Code, to add National Korean War Veterans Armistice Day to the list of days on which the flag should especially be displayed.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Korean War Veterans Recognition Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Display of flag on National Korean War Veterans Armistice Day

Section 6(d) of title 4, United States Code, is amended by inserting “National Korean War Veterans Armistice Day, July 27;” after “July 4;”.
