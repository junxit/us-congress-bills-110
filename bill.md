# H.R. 4046

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to require the Department of Education to accept certifications of permanent and total disability by the Department of Veterans Affairs for the purpose of student loan discharge.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Student Disability Fairness Act”.

## § 2. Disability Determinations

Section 437(a) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1087(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following new sentence: “In making such determination of permanent and total disability, the Secretary shall provide that a borrower who has been certified as permanently and totally disabled by the Department of Veterans Affairs shall not be required to present further documentation for purposes of this title.”.
