# S. 2816

> To provide for the appointment of the Chief Human Capital Officer of the Department of Homeland Security by the Secretary of Homeland Security.

## § 1. Appointment of the Chief Human Capital Officer by the Secretary of Homeland Security

Section 103(d) of the Homeland Security Act of 2002 (6 U.S.C. 113(d)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking paragraph (3); and
- **(2)** redesignating paragraphs (4) and (5) as paragraphs (3) and (4), respectively.
