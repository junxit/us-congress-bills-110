# H.R. 4224

> To prohibit the Secretary of State from making a contribution to the United Nations until such time as the United Nations is in compliance with fire, building, and safety codes.

## § 1. Withholding of contributions to the United Nations for building noncompliance

The Secretary of State may not make a contribution to any regularly assessed biennial budget of the United Nations unless the Secretary has—

- **(1)** confirmed with the Mayor of the City of New York that United Nations facilities located in the City are in full compliance with all fire, building, and safety codes; and
- **(2)** submitted to Congress a certification with respect to a confirmation under paragraph (1).
