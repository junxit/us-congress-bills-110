# H.R. 6651

> To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to decrease the period of benefit ineligibility of certain adults during their unemployment.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Food Security Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Amendments

- **(1)** *Modification of ineligibility* Section 6(o) of the Food Stamp Act of 1977 (7 U.S.C. 2015(o)) is amended—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (2) by striking “3 months” and inserting “12 months”,
  - **(B)** in paragraph (5)(C) by striking “3-month” each place it appears and inserting “12-month”, and
  - **(C)** in paragraph (6)(A)(ii)(IV) by striking “3 months” and inserting “12 months”.
- **(2)** *Conforming amendment* Section 16(h)(1)(E)(ii)(I) is amended by striking “3-month” and inserting “12-month”.
