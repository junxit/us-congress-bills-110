# S. 2131

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at Highway 49 South in Piney Woods, Mississippi, as the “Laurence C. and Grace M. Jones Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Laurence C. and Grace M. Jones Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at Highway 49 South in Piney Woods, Mississippi, shall be known and designated as the “Laurence C. and Grace M. Jones Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Laurence C. and Grace M. Jones Post Office Building”.
