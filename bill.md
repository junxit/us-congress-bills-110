# H.R. 95

> To make funds available for fiscal years 2007 and 2008 for program integrity purposes, including the data mining project, under the Federal Crop Insurance Act.

## § 1. Crop insurance program integrity

For each of fiscal years 2007 and 2008, the Secretary of Agriculture may use not more than $3,600,000 of funds made available under section 522(e) of the Federal Crop Insurance Act (7 U.S.C. 1522(e)) for program integrity purposes, including the data mining project.
