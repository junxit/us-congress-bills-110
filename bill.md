# H.R. 700

> To amend the Federal Water Pollution Control Act to extend the pilot program for alternative water source projects.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Healthy Communities Water Supply Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Pilot program for alternative water source projects

Section 220(j) of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1300(j)) is amended by striking “$75,000,000 for fiscal years 2002 through 2004” and inserting “$125,000,000”.
