# S. 3010

> To reauthorize the Route 66 Corridor Preservation Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Route 66 Corridor Preservation Program Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Route 66 Corridor Preservation Program

Section 4 of Public Law 106–45 (16 U.S.C. 461 note; 113 Stat. 226) is amended by striking “2009” and inserting “2019”.
