# S. 606

> To improve Federal contracting and procurement by eliminating fraud and abuse and improving competition in contracting and procurement and by enhancing administration of Federal contracting personnel, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Honest Leadership and Accountability in Contracting Act of 2007”.
