# H.R. 784

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to change the effective date for paid-up coverage under the military Survivor Benefit Plan.

## § 1. Earlier effective date for paid-up coverage under survivor benefit plan

Section 1452(j) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking “October 1, 2008” and inserting “October 1, 2007”.
