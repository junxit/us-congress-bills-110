# S. 2818

> To amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 and the Public Health Service Act to provide for enhanced health insurance marketplace pooling and relating market rating.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Business Health Plans Act of 2008”.
