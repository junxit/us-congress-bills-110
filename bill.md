# H.R. 1753

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make the expensing of environmental remediation costs permanent law and to repeal the recapture of such expenses under section 1245 of such Code.

## § 1. EXPENSING OF ENVIRONMENTAL REMEDIATION COSTS MADE PERMANENT; REPEAL OF SECTION 1245 RECAPTURE

- **(a)** *Provision made permanent* Section 198 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to expensing of environmental remediation costs) is amended by striking subsection (h).
- **(b)** *Elimination of Recapture on Sale* Section 198 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking subsection (e) and by redesignating subsections (f) and (g) as subsections (e) and (f), respectively.
- **(c)** *Effective dates*
  - **(1)** *Subsection (a)* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to expenditures paid or incurred after December 31, 2006.
  - **(2)** *Subsection (b)* The amendment made by subsection (b) shall apply to dispositions after the date of the enactment of this Act.
