# H.R. 6003

> To reauthorize Amtrak, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Passenger Rail Investment and Improvement Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Amendment of title 49, United States Code

Except as otherwise specifically provided, whenever in this Act an amendment is expressed in terms of an amendment to a section or other provision of law, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of title 49, United States Code.
