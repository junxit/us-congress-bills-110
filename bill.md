# S. 3510

> To prohibit the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System from making funds available at a discount rate to private individuals, partnerships, and corporations.

## § 1. Repeal of discount authority for private firms

Section 13 of the Federal Reserve Act (12 U.S.C. 343) is amended by striking the third undesignated paragraph (relating to discounts for individuals, partnerships, and corporations).
