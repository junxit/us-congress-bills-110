# H.R. 4163

> To provide an enhanced penalty for threatening to kill, injure, or intimidate an individual, or to cause property damage, by means of fire or an explosive on school property.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Zero Tolerance School Terror Act”.

## § 2. Enhanced penalty for threatening to kill, injure, or intimidate an individual, or to cause property damage, by means of fire or an explosive on school property

Section 844(e) of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following: “If the threat or conveying of false information referred to in the preceding sentence concerns conduct on the grounds of a school which provides elementary or secondary education (as determined under State law), the maximum term of imprisonment under this subsection shall be 20 years.”.
