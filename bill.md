# H.R. 568

> To amend section 255 of the National Housing Act to remove the limitation on the number of reverse mortgages that may be insured under the FHA mortgage insurance program for such mortgages.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reverse Mortgages to Help America’s Seniors Act”.

## § 2. Elimination of cap on number of mortgages insured

Section 255 of the National Housing Act (12 U.S.C. 1715z–20) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (g), by striking the first sentence; and
- **(2)** in subsection (i)(1)(C), by striking “limitations” and inserting “limitation”.
