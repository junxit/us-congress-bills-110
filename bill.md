# S. 1548

> To authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2008 for military activities of the Department of Defense, to prescribe military personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Department of Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2008”.
