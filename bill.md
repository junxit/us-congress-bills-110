# H.R. 6597

> To require the collection of data on animal cruelty crimes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Animal Cruelty Statistics Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Data on animal cruelty crimes

Not later than one year after the date of the enactment of this Act the Attorney General shall make appropriate changes to existing crime data bases maintained within the Department of Justice so that data on all crimes of animal cruelty will be collected and made publicly available in a manner that facilitates analysis.
