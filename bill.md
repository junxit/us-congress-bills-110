# H.R. 5078

> To extend the suspension of duty on Desmodur BL XP 2468.

## § 1. Desmodur BL XP 2468

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.12.12 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Copolymer of methyl ethyl ketoxime and toluene­di­iso­cya­nate) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
