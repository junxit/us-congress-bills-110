# H.R. 5349

> To extend the Protect America Act of 2007 for 21 days.

## § 1. 21-day extension of the Protect America Act of 2007

Section 6(c) of the Protect America Act of 2007 (Public Law 110–55; 121 Stat. 557; 50 U.S.C. 1803 note) is amended by striking “195 days” and inserting “216 days”.
