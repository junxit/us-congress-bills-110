# S. 3478

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide incentives for the production of energy, to provide transportation and domestic fuel security, and to provide incentives for energy conservation and energy efficiency, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title, etc

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Energy Independence and Investment Act of 2008”.
- **(b)** *Reference* Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
