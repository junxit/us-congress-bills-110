# H.R. 2369

> To extend the authorization of appropriations for the Office of Government Ethics through fiscal year 2011.

## § 1. Extension of authorization of appropriations for Office of Government Ethics

Section 405 of the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. App.) is amended by striking “fiscal year 2007” and inserting “each of fiscal years 2008 through 2011”.
