# S. 2700

> To amend the Oil Pollution Act of 1990 to double the liability limits for single-hull tankers and tank bargers for 2009, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Increased single-hull liability limits for 2009

Section 1004(a)(1)(A) of the Oil Pollution Act of 1990 (33 U.S.C. 2704) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(A)** with respect to a single-hull vessel, including a single-hull vessel fitted with double sides only or a double bottom—
>   - **(i)** only $3,000 per gross ton, if the incident for which liability is imposed under section 1002 occurs before January 1, 2009; and
>   - **(ii)** only $6,000 per gross ton, if the incident for which liability is imposed under section 1002 occurs after December 31, 2009, and before January 1, 2010;
