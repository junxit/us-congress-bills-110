# H.R. 3976

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent the deduction for contributions of food inventory by all corporations.

## § 1. Deduction for contributions of food inventory by all corporations made permanent

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (C) of section 170(e)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to special rule for certain contributions of inventory and other property) is amended by striking clause (iv).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to made after December 31, 2007.
