# H.R. 3736

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent the election to treat combat pay as earned income for purposes of the earned income tax credit.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Combat Pay Tax Flexibility Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Election to treat combat pay as earned income for purposes of the earned income tax credit made permanent

- **(a)** *In general* Clause (vi) of section 32(c)(2)(B) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (defining earned income) is amended to read as follows:
  > - **(vi)** a taxpayer may elect to treat amounts excluded from gross income by reason of section 112 as earned income.

- **(b)** *Sunset not applicable* Section 105 of the Working Families Tax Relief Act of 2004 (relating to application of EGTRRA sunset to this title) shall not apply to section 104(b) of such Act.
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to taxable years ending after December 31, 2007.
