# S. 1826

> To add Kentucky State University to the list of schools eligible for assistance under part B of title III of the Higher Education Act of 1965.

## § 1. Kentucky state university qualified graduate program

Section 326(e)(1) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1063b(e)(1)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (Q), by striking “and” after the semicolon;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (R), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(S)** Kentucky State University qualified graduate program.
