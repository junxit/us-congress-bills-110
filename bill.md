# H.R. 778

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent the residential energy efficient property credit.

## § 1. Residential energy efficient property credit made permanent

Section 25D of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to residential energy efficient property) is amended by striking subsection (g).
