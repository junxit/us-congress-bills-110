# H.R. 3735

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the look-through treatment of payments between related controlled foreign corporations.

## § 1. Look-through treatment of payments between related controlled foreign corporations

Subparagraph (B) of section 954(c)(6) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to look-thru rule for related controlled foreign corporations) is amended by striking “January 1, 2009” and inserting “January 1, 2014”.
