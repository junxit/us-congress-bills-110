# S. 279

> To repeal certain sections of the Act of May 26, 1936, pertaining to the Virgin Islands.

## § 1. Repeal of certain laws pertaining to the Virgin Islands

- **(a)** *Repeal* Sections 1 through 6 of the Act of May 26, 1936 (48 U.S.C. 1401 et seq.), are repealed.
- **(b)** *Effective Date* The amendment made by this section takes effect on July 22, 1954.
