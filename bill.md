# H.R. 1124

> To extend the District of Columbia College Access Act of 1999.

## § 1. 5–Year Reauthorization of Tuition Assistance Programs

- **(a)** *Public School Program* Section 3(i) of the District of Columbia College Access Act of 1999 (sec. 38–2702(i), DC Official Code) is amended by striking “each of the 7 succeeding fiscal years” and inserting “each of the 12 succeeding fiscal years”.
- **(b)** *Private School Program* Section 5(f) of such Act (sec. 38–2704(f), DC Official Code) is amended by striking “each of the 7 succeeding fiscal years” and inserting “each of the 12 succeeding fiscal years”.
