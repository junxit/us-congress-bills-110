# H.R. 2433

> To prohibit the designation of any agency, bureau, or other entity of the Department of Homeland Security as a separate agency or bureau for purposes of post employment restrictions in title 18, United States Code.

## § 1. Integrity in post-employment

- **(a)** *Designations as separate agencies and bureaus barred* No agency, bureau, or other entity of the Department of Homeland Security may be designated under section 207(h)(1) of title 18, United States Code, as a separate agency or bureau.
- **(b)** *Effective Date*
  - **(1)** *In general* This section takes effect on the date of the enactment of this Act.
  - **(2)** *Applicability to designations* The following shall cease to be effective on the date of the enactment of this Act:
    - **(A)** Any waiver of restrictions made under section 207(c)(2)(C) of title 18, United States Code, before the enactment of this Act, with respect to any position, or category of positions, in the Department of Homeland Security.
    - **(B)** Any designation of an agency, bureau, or other entity in the Department of Homeland Security, before the enactment of this Act, under section 207(h)(1) of title 18, United States Code, as a separate agency or bureau.
