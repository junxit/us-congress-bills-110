# H.R. 6913

> To provide that no funds made available to the Department of Commerce may be used to implement, administer, or enforce certain amendments made to regulations relating to license exemptions for gift parcels and humanitarian donations for Cuba.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Securing Eligibility for Needed Donations Act of 2008” or the “SEND Act of 2008”.

## § 2. Limitation on use of funds to implement, administer, or enforce certain amendments made to regulations relating to license exemptions for gift parcels and humanitarian donations for Cuba

- **(a)** *Limitation* No funds made available to the Department of Commerce may be used to implement, administer, or enforce the amendments made to section 740.12 of title 15, Code of Federal Regulations (relating to license exemptions for gift parcels and humanitarian donations for Cuba), as published in the Federal Register on June 22, 2004 (69 Fed. Reg. 34565–34567).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The limitation on use of funds in subsection (a) shall take effect on the date of the enactment of this Act and shall apply with respect to the 90-day period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act.
