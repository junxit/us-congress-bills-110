# H.R. 1239

> To amend the National Underground Railroad Network to Freedom Act of 1998 to authorize additional funding to carry out the Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Underground Railroad Network to Freedom Amendments Act of 2007”.

## § 2. Authorizing appropriations for specific purposes

The National Underground Railroad Network to Freedom Act of 1998 (16 U.S.C. 469l et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking section 3(d);
- **(2)** by striking section 4(d); and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 5. Authorization of appropriations
  >
  > - **(a)** *Amounts* There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this Act $2,500,000 for each fiscal year, to be allocated as follows:
  >   - **(1)** $2,000,000 is to be used for the purposes of section 3.
  >   - **(2)** $500,000 is to be used for the purposes of section 4.
  > - **(b)** *Restrictions* No amounts may be appropriated for the purposes of this Act except to the Secretary for carrying out the responsibilities of the Secretary as set forth in this Act.

## § 3. Effective Date

The amendments made by section 2 shall take effect at the beginning of the fiscal year immediately following the date of the enactment of this Act.
