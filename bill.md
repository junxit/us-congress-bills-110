# H.R. 4378

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on DMDS.

## § 1. DMDS

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.33.92 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to 2,2-Dithiobis(8-fluoro-5-methoxy)-1,2,4-triazolo[1,5-c]pyrimidine) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “Free” and inserting “1%”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall take effect on January 1, 2010.
