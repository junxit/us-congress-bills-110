# H.R. 3721

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1190 Lorena Road in Lorena, Texas, as the “Marine Gunnery Sgt. John D. Fry Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Marine Gunnery Sgt. John D. Fry Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1190 Lorena Road in Lorena, Texas, shall be known and designated as the “Marine Gunnery Sgt. John D. Fry Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Marine Gunnery Sgt. John D. Fry Post Office Building”.
