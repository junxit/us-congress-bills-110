# H.R. 1315

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to provide specially adaptive housing assistance to certain disabled members of the Armed Forces residing temporarily in housing owned by a family member.

## § 1. Provision of specially adaptive housing assistance to disabled members of the Armed Forces residing temporarily in housing owned by a family member

Section 2102A of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subsections (d) and (e) as subsections (e) and (f), respectively; and
- **(2)** by inserting after subsection (c) the following new subsection (d):
  > - **(c)** *Assistance for members of the Armed Forces* The Secretary may provide assistance under subsection (a) to a member of the Armed Forces serving on active duty who is suffering from a disability described in subsection (a)(2) or (b)(2) of section 2101 of this title if such disability is the result of an injury incurred or disease contracted in or aggravated in the line of duty in the active military, naval, or air service. Such assistance shall be provided to the same extent as assistance is provided under subsection (a) to veterans eligible for assistance under that subsection and subject to the limitation under subsection (c).
