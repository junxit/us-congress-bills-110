# H.R. 6046

> To extend the authorization provided in the Act titled “An Act to preserve the cultural resources of the Route 66 corridor and to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to provide assistance”.

## § 1. Authorization extended

Section 4 of the Act titled “An Act to preserve the cultural resources of the Route 66 corridor and to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to provide assistance” (16 U.S.C. 461 note) is amended by striking “2009” and inserting “2019”.
