# H.R. 1151

> To provide for a temporary increase in the number of Iraqi and Afghan translators in the United States Armed Forces who may be provided status as special immigrants.

## § 1. Temporary increase in number of Iraqi and Afghan translators in the United States Armed Forces who may be provided status as special immigrants

Section 1059(c)(1) of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2006 (Public Law 109–163) is amended by inserting before the period at the end the following: “(or 300 in the case of each of fiscal years 2007, 2008, and 2009)”.
