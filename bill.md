# S. 2936

> To amend title XXI of the Social Security Act to reauthorize the State Children's Health Insurance Program, to limit income eligibility expansions under that program until the lowest income eligible individuals are enrolled, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; references; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Responsible Expansion of the State Children's Health Insurance Program Act of 2008”.
- **(b)** *References to SCHIP; Secretary* In this Act:
  - **(1)** *SCHIP* The term SCHIP means the State Children’s Health Insurance Program established under title XXI of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1397aa et seq.).
  - **(2)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of Health and Human Services.
