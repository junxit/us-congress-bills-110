# H.R. 5043

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on Phenmedipham.

## § 1. Phenmedipham

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended—
  - **(1)** in heading 9902.13.76, by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2011”; and
  - **(2)** by striking heading 9902.31.13.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a)(1) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
