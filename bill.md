# H.R. 329

> To amend title 40, United States Code, to designate certain counties as part of the Appalachian region.

## § 1. Additional counties in appalachian region

Section 14102(a)(1)(L) of title 40, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “Franklin” after “Floyd”;
- **(2)** by inserting “Henry,” after “Grayson,”; and
- **(3)** by inserting “Patrick,” after “Montgomery,”.
