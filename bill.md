# H. CON. RES. 358

> Commending the members of the Nevada Army National Guard and Air National Guard for their service to the State of Nevada and the United States.

## Preamble

- Whereas the 100 Nevadans of the 72nd Military Police Company, based in Henderson, Nevada, bravely served in Iraq from May 2003 to November 2003;
- Whereas the 120 Nevadans of the 321st Signal Company, based in Reno, Nevada, bravely served in Kuwait and Iraq from December 2003 to March 2005;
- Whereas the 50 Nevadans of the 777th Engineer Company, based in Henderson, Nevada, bravely served in Kuwait from May 2003 to May 2004;
- Whereas the 140 Nevadans of the 1864th Transportation Company, based in Henderson, Nevada, bravely served in Kuwait and Iraq from July 2004 to November 2005;
- Whereas the 500 Nevadans of the 1/221st Cavalry, based in Las Vegas, Nevada, bravely served at Fort Irwin, California from July 2004 to July 2006;
- Whereas the 100 Nevadans of D Company, 113th Aviation, based in Stead, Nevada, bravely served in Afghanistan from January 2005 to June 2006;
- Whereas the 10 Nevadans of Detachment 45, OSA, based in Stead, Nevada, bravely served in Kuwait from February 2005 to October 2005;
- Whereas the 100 Nevadans of the 593rd Transportation Company, based in Stead, Nevada, bravely served in Iraq from July 2006 to October 2007;
- Whereas the 110 Nevadans of the selected members of the 1/221st Cavalry, based in Las Vegas, Nevada, bravely served in Iraq from May 2006 to July 2007;
- Whereas the 110 Nevadans of the 72nd Military Police Company, based in Henderson, Nevada, bravely served in Iraq from November 2007 to February 2008;
- Whereas the 12 Nevadans of the 140th Military Police Detachment, based in Henderson, Nevada, bravely served in Iraq from February 2008 to April 2008;
- Whereas the 40 Nevadans of the 152nd Airlift Wing and 152nd Intelligence Squadron, based in Reno, Nevada, bravely served in Iraq from September 2005 to September 2006;
- Whereas the 30 Nevadans of the 192nd Airlift Squadron and 152nd Mait Squadron, based in Reno, Nevada, bravely served in Puerto Rico from July 2005 to October 2005;
- Whereas the 45 Nevadans of the 152nd Airlift Wing and 152nd Intelligence Squadron, based in Reno, Nevada, bravely served in Iraq from February 2007 to March 2008;
- Whereas the 20 Nevadans of the 232nd Operations Squadron, based in Las Vegas, Nevada, bravely serve in current operations in Afghanistan and Iraq; and
- Whereas the 15 Nevadans of various units of the Nevada Air National Guard, based in Reno, Nevada, bravely served in Iraq from February 2007 to March 2008: Now, therefore, be it

That Congress commends the members of the Nevada Army National Guard and Air National Guard for their brave service to the State of Nevada and the United States.
